Technical Findings & Takeaways

1. Why AV1 Needs 2.3x Higher Bitrate and 60ms Latency: To match PointStream's joint accuracy, AV1 must encode at native 1080p (672 kbps), requiring 2.3x higher network bandwidth and 60.2 ms encode latency, which breaks real-time 50 ms teleoperation constraints.

2. Static Keyframe Plates vs Continuous Motion Compensation: Transmitting an infilled keyframe plate every 2 seconds was empirically evaluated. Because egocentric cameras move continuously with worker head motion, static plates result in 11.0 dB PSNR and 0.454 LPIPS. In contrast, continuous SVT-AV1 inter-frame coding leverages motion vectors to preserve background fidelity (23.4 dB PSNR) at one-third the bitrate.

3. Transparent Bitrate & Latency Accounting: PointStream's ~290 kbps budget explicitly accounts for all three streams: background (262 kbps), appearance anchors (23 kbps amortized), and 3D keypoints (8.4 kbps). Latency includes background CPU encoding (13.6 ms) in both parallel (17.9 ms) and serial (31.5 ms) operating modes.
