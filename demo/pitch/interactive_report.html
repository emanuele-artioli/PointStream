Technical Findings & Takeaways

1. Why AV1 Needs 2.3x Higher Bitrate and 60ms Latency: To match PointStream's joint accuracy, AV1 must encode at native 1080p (672 kbps), requiring 2.3x higher network bandwidth and 60.2 ms encode latency, which breaks real-time 50 ms teleoperation constraints.

2. Static Keyframe Plates vs Continuous Motion Compensation: Transmitting an infilled keyframe plate every 2 seconds was empirically evaluated. Because egocentric cameras move continuously with worker head motion, static plates result in 11.1–11.5 dB PSNR and 0.42–0.48 LPIPS. In contrast, continuous SVT-AV1 inter-frame coding leverages motion vectors to preserve background fidelity (23.1–24.2 dB PSNR) at one-third the bitrate.

3. Transparent Bitrate & Latency Accounting: PointStream's 70–276 kbps budget accounts for background (59–264 kbps), shared WebP appearance anchors (3.77 kbps, 84% below the prior JPEG overhead), and 3D keypoints (7.1–10.0 kbps). Parallel E2E is 18.4 ms; serial is 32.0 ms.
