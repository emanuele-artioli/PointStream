# PointStream paper figures

PointStream splits a broadcast at cuts. For a suitable shot it can send points describing an object’s motion, appearance where needed, and a background. Other shots use a conventional codec. The viewer receives one timeline. An application can read the object data from the same delivery. Keep the design general through the system section and evaluate the choices on tennis.

## November 22 manuscript and confirmation

The revised abstract leads with foreground at a common scene budget. With two independently decoded development rallies and 23 source-reviewed instants the equal-rally foreground DISTS is 0.290 for PointStream, 0.428 for affordable ordinary AV1 and 0.350 for DCVC-UF under a valid appearance cache. The three-match historical player study, prospective Shanghai ball comparison and measured component costs belong in the main text. The reference figure uses objective generated-body distortion across shared settings. Author preference is supplementary.

**How much independent evidence to target.** The twelve-match ball test represents twenty players. The local archive has 159 original scenes from five verified matches and a sixth full-match source, Nadal–Kyrgios. A seventh archive label reverses two player names and contains existing encodes. Its identity needs confirmation before it can add an independent event. The RacketVision official test split contains 43 clips. Count separate rallies within a broadcast only when the source establishes that a different point was played. [Source inventory](docs/experiments/cohort-expansion-20261122/sampling.md) For a broad player or joint-foreground claim, target at least three prespecified rallies from each of six distinct matches (18 complete delivery decisions), including at least two held-out events. Select by source and stratify on camera scale and court type before viewing outputs. Keep every fallback in the denominator. Compare within the same shot, then average within match and show the per-match effects and uncertainty. More ticks on either development rally improve its measurement but supply no new match. This is a design target, not a power calculation or a journal requirement. Target independent labels on all sampled sources. RacketVision supplies ball and racket marks, while the two-player body masks need separate annotation. The published [Tennis Player Actions Dataset](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11282921/) offers hand-labeled joints on self-recorded action images; those images can test anatomical localization but do not supply body ground truth for these broadcast packets. At the measured Sinner–Shelton speed the two synthesis tracks cost about 39 GPU-minutes for a 240-frame shot. Eighteen equally costly generated shots would take roughly 11.7 GPU-hours for synthesis alone, before controls or fallback, and several bitrate settings can reuse the resulting player bank.

## Argument for the rewrite

Open on a rally. The reader should see what the viewer sees, what the server actually sends and what an application can recover from those same bytes. Then ask which mode gives the most useful foreground at a given total bitrate, how the choice changes with receiver state, and how much computation playback requires.

The last submission led with a 7–10× saving against ultrafast HEVC and advance delivery of player models. Reviewers asked about novelty, stronger baselines, convincing motion and changing backgrounds. Cite Lai et al.’s early tennis shot decomposition and coordinate-map representation in the introduction. Explain PointStream’s reconstructed broadcast and its direct machine interface. Discuss MPEG-4 sprites and AV1 long-term references at the court-return figure. The existing conventional reference experiment saved 4.84% at equal whole-passage luma quality on its test passage. ## Results from the preceding overnight campaign

The preceding campaign ran from 21:24 November 21 to 09:24 November 22 UTC on campus. [Current results](RESEARCH.md) give the numbers; the experiment cards preserve every job, bound and acceptance test.

1. **A complete low-rate stream.** Completed the two cleaned-base settings with both bodies, the ball and actual headers. The same-frame AV1, ROI and DCVC-UF controls establish the cold and warm rates, foreground fidelity, motion and receiver time. [Packet and controls](docs/experiments/joint-trajectory-20261122/README.md)
2. **Multi-match evidence.** The three-codec player curve covers seven windows on three matches. A second, complete joint receiver now has measured prices near 11, 20 and 40 kbps on Alcaraz–Hurkacz. The AV1 control spans three resolutions. The author reviewed all twelve original source instants. The two-rally joint comparison now uses 23 instants with a visible ball and both bodies. [Scoring and denominators](docs/experiments/reviewed-foreground-20261122/README.md) [Player control jobs](docs/experiments/player-rd-historical-20261122/README.md) · [Alcaraz–Hurkacz replication](docs/experiments/joint-replication-feasibility-20261122/README.md)
3. **Machine tasks at the same price.** Compared decoded frames and direct coordinates on the joint serve, the dense clip, Adelaide and Shanghai. In Shanghai, incomplete player conditioning routed both bodies to AV1. The ball-only stream improved ball-crop DISTS by 13.4% over matched AV1 and directly localized 18/26 visible balls. The decoded-video detector missed its source accuracy threshold. [Dense machine test](docs/experiments/ball-matched-low-20261122/README.md) · [Adelaide](docs/experiments/ball-adelaide-prospective-20261122/README.md) · [Shanghai](docs/experiments/shanghai-prospective-20261122/README.md)
4. **Receiver state across cuts.** The receiver replayed both visits and the intervening footage. PointStream paid 2.15% fewer bytes than AV1 with identical cuts. Continuous AV1 delivered higher whole-picture fidelity at a lower rate. The two object-coded visits were selected retrospectively. [Passage](docs/experiments/cumulative-joint-20261122/README.md) · [Route audit](docs/experiments/passage-routing-20261122/README.md)
5. **Explain reference and background choices.** Tested shared AVIF qualities and reuse after a cutaway. On the return both reused appearances were barely acceptable. A segmented, donor-filled court reduced base bytes by 18.9% at fixed CRF on one shot, with visible residual fragments. Keep equipment separate until its fidelity is measured. ## Working from figures

Draft a caption before launching a paper experiment. Name the missing point, metric, denominator, comparator, byte accounting and the decision the result would change. Look for existing receipts first. Publish actual operating points, scope comparisons to common support and retain contrary outcomes beside the relevant claim. Build the author book with `python docs/paper/figures/review.py NEW_REVIEW.pdf` and add `--main-only` for the proposed manuscript sequence. The optional private images need publication clearance.
