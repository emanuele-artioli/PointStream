## Decision boundary

The development screen may run two development windows per domain after its
dependencies pass: ego is prioritized and tennis remains in scope. It compares
complete PointStream with uniform AV1/VVC, verified conventional ROI, simple
paste/genuine warp, and a published neural anchor. The screen is not
confirmation and does not waive the original tennis-primary, neural-foreground
winner, or second-domain requirement. At the 20 September checkpoint, record
the adopted claim/domain and whether that neural-winner requirement is retained
or explicitly revised before any confirmation freeze.

All comparisons account for `T = B + F + M + R + H + declared deployment`, use
the same pixels/container/timebase, report bytes, quality and encode/client
time, and retain source-level uncertainty. No BD-rate follows from nonoverlap.
Stop and return a cost card if headroom/budget is absent, an instrument fails,
or repeats consume the cap.
