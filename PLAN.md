# PointStream paper figures

PointStream splits a broadcast at cuts. For a suitable shot it can send points describing an object’s motion, appearance where needed, and a background. Other shots use a conventional codec. The viewer receives one timeline. An application can read the object data from the same delivery. Keep the design general through the system section. Use tennis for the main evaluation and carry the measured boundary conditions from other domains in the discussion and appendix.

## November 22 manuscript and confirmation

The revised abstract leads with foreground at a common scene budget. With two independently decoded development rallies and 23 source-reviewed instants the equal-rally foreground DISTS is 0.290 for PointStream, 0.428 for affordable ordinary AV1 and 0.350 for DCVC-UF under a valid appearance cache. The three-match historical player study, prospective Shanghai ball comparison and measured component costs belong in the main text. The reference figure uses objective generated-body distortion across shared settings. Author preference is supplementary.

**How much independent evidence to target.** The twelve-match ball test represents twenty players. The local archive has 159 original scenes from five verified matches and a sixth full-match source, Nadal–Kyrgios. A seventh archive label reverses two player names and contains existing encodes. Its identity needs confirmation before it can add an independent event. The RacketVision official test split contains 43 clips. Count separate rallies within a broadcast only when the source establishes that a different point was played. [Source inventory](docs/experiments/cohort-expansion-20261122/sampling.md) For a broad player or joint-foreground claim, target at least three prespecified rallies from each of six distinct matches (18 complete delivery decisions), including at least two held-out events. Select by source and stratify on camera scale and court type before viewing outputs. Keep every fallback in the denominator. Compare within the same shot, then average within match and show the per-match effects and uncertainty. More ticks on either development rally improve its measurement but supply no new match. This is a design target, not a power calculation or a journal requirement. Target independent labels on all sampled sources. RacketVision supplies ball and racket marks, while the two-player body masks need separate annotation. The published [Tennis Player Actions Dataset](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11282921/) offers hand-labeled joints on self-recorded action images; those images can test anatomical localization but do not supply body ground truth for these broadcast packets. At the measured Sinner–Shelton speed the two synthesis tracks cost about 39 GPU-minutes for a 240-frame shot. Eighteen equally costly generated shots would take roughly 11.7 GPU-hours for synthesis alone, before controls or fallback, and several bitrate settings can reuse the resulting player bank.

## Argument for the rewrite

Open on a rally. The reader should see what the viewer sees, what the server actually sends and what an application can recover from those same bytes. Then ask which mode gives the most useful foreground at a given total bitrate, how the choice changes with receiver state, and how much computation playback requires.

The last submission led with a 7–10× saving against ultrafast HEVC and advance delivery of player models. Reviewers asked about novelty, stronger baselines, convincing motion and changing backgrounds. Cite Lai et al.’s early tennis shot decomposition and coordinate-map representation in the introduction. Explain PointStream’s reconstructed broadcast and its direct machine interface. Discuss MPEG-4 sprites and AV1 long-term references at the court-return figure. The existing conventional reference experiment saved 4.84% at equal whole-passage luma quality on its test passage. The preceding campaign ran from 21:24 November 21 to 09:24 November 22 UTC on campus. [Current results](RESEARCH.md) give the numbers; the experiment cards preserve every job, bound and acceptance test.

3. **Machine tasks at the same price.** Compared decoded frames and direct coordinates on the joint serve, the dense clip, Adelaide and Shanghai.
