# W8 exact-source CPU proof proposal — not released

Purpose: qualify the new W8 witness source, strict worker policy and process teardown before any new GPU request. This is synthetic infrastructure and packet/native correctness evidence, not generation, kernel, quality or full-witness evidence. No execution, upload or release is authorized by this proposal. Root must review the complete frozen entrypoint, fleet spec, source and cost model before any submission.

## Bound and admission

Reserve at most **800 CPU-s and 0 GPU-s**, wholly inside the existing 4,550 CPU-s W8 phase: at most 50 CPU-s shared fresh readiness/source preparation, at most 600 CPU-s for the one-core fleet campaign, at most 100 CPU-s for supervisor/claim release and publication-to-canonical-terminal completion after the campaign, and at most 50 CPU-s independent audit/closeout. These are distinct caps, not measured usage. The campaign must inherit a verified single CPU affinity throughout the owned tree, enforce a hard deadline (at most 45 minutes from release), hard wall and per-process CPU limits, capture waited child CPU and process identities, and stop before a stage whose upper bound cannot fit. Charge the conservative whole one-core lifecycle wall plus separately bounded preparation/audit; pending wall time is not CPU time, but queue-watch and administration CPU remain in the external allowances; do not claim aggregate CPU was measured from one parent counter. Preserve all partial artifacts, and inspect an uncertain submission rather than replaying it.

The prospective smoke allocation is 80 seconds packet worker + 90 seconds synthetic namespace + 120 seconds MAIN/JCV policy + 45 seconds independent assessment role-policy probe + 135 seconds for three native invocations + 20 seconds packet/setup overhead + 30 seconds publication and cleanup = 520 seconds, leaving 10 seconds within the 530-second smoke. Each native allowance includes its 20-second command bound and 25-second outer reserve; the campaign separately reserves 50 seconds validator, 5 seconds completion marker and 15 seconds slack. These are caps, not measured times, and any admission/remaining-time failure stops. The 100-second post-campaign allowance includes the observed prior approximately 60-second interval with a 1.5× margin (approximately 90 seconds), not an assertion that this source will match it.

The proof-only packet worker cap is 80 seconds, leaving the legacy 130-second path unchanged. The prior 6.531-second worker observation ×1.5 is approximately 9.80 seconds; adding 30 seconds for cold local runtime/setup, 10 for namespace cleanup and 20 for other verification gives approximately 69.8 seconds. These are prospective allowances, not measurements for the new source; admission requires local packed runtimes and staged input caches, and the proof stops if the cap fails. Remaining-time checks reserve only unfinished work.

Prior evidence informs feasibility but is not a guarantee: packet job `20261105T051531Z-bd0b5915` had a packet worker at 6.426 CPU-s/6.531 wall-s, though its original fleet validator failed and only independent artifact audit accepted the substantive checks. The earlier native inventory v4 completed in 9.9435 wall-s and its independent audit rehashed the pinned 47,001,600-byte YUV, but it did not establish restricted execution or this new source. The new proof retains the 47,001,600-byte YUV for independent rehash; its additional copying and publication must fit the reserved publication/cleanup allowance. Cold filesystem, source staging, protected-target stat checks, native repetitions and strict policy proof may dominate. Measure or conservatively cap them; if the actual combined forecast does not fit the 600-second campaign and its 530-second smoke, stop and obtain a revised prospective reservation rather than shrinking scientific or cleanup checks.

## Required actual checks

1. Exercise the exact production reaper with its fixed CPU-only synthetic harness: normal, timeout, TERM and KILL; in every case observe and retain host PID, start ticks, process group and PID namespace identity for namespace init and worker, plus the synthetic detached child, and verify their disappearance. Normal exit must record init and waited child CPU and agree with independently waited outer-child CPU within the declared 0.25-second synthetic-workload tolerance. Timeout/TERM/KILL must retain cause and any pre-exit identity receipt. The kernel PID-namespace teardown supports the bounded descendant containment claim; do not claim an individually observed inventory of unknown descendants.
2. Run the exact fixed native CPU target under that same reaper: one normal real FFmpeg/libdav1d invocation with the pinned IVF, exact binary/loader/library inventory and expected YUV SHA `7490edf9b678db805c23baae49a2a3b5e9d7b037b18dd216320aad47a061beff`, plus separate bounded timeout and SIGKILL cases. Record trusted init/bootstrap host PID/startticks/namespace and verify their post-stop absence. The early namespace identity record must exist before interruption; missing or malformed identity, live owner or unverified cleanup fails the proof. Native execution may use its own session inside the PID namespace; no native GPU is allowed.
3. Run the new source's actual isolated CPU packet path with original packet and identity gates: exact decoded checkpoint and 480 individual name/shape/dtype/raw-byte fingerprints, both packet unit NPZ hashes, native packet framing, typed stat-only protected-target identity and 10 denial probes, seven mutation denials, exact source manifest, runtime grants and preserved canaries. The recorded SHA values are historical references only: no protected source, golden, prepared, fit or mask content is opened or rehashed before full producer promotion. Verify CUDA remains hidden and uninitialized. Verify the common MAIN/JCV runtime policy and exact 12 approved system leaves plus bound clock-helper source/policy using genuine Linux namespace/Landlock calls, not a mocked policy or synthetic successful JSON. New mode routing and grant sets are static/unit-tested; this does not assert that each GPU mode executed. A separate fresh PID-namespace CPU leaf must run the actual privileged assessment Landlock helper on synthetic fixture inputs, proving input-write and undeclared-read/execute denials, output writes, and the pinned ffmpeg version invocation; this is a role-policy component check, not a candidate approval or scoring result. The proof must cover qualification and full-witness dispatch without generating model outputs; any policy operation requiring CUDA is instead a separately admitted GPU gate and must not be claimed as a CPU pass.
4. Independently validate and seal the exact raw receipts, source/spec/input hashes, runtime/host, stage statuses, affinity/lifetime/cost records and cleanup evidence. The current WIP `w8_witness_job.check_cpu_gate` expects schema 1, scope `root-reviewed W8 same-source CPU isolation and native lifecycle proof`, `passed: true`, `source_sha256`, `snapshot_sha256`, `revision`, `environment_sha256`, `host`, actual `job_id` and `spec_sha256`. Its `raw_receipt_sha256` must contain distinct SHA256s for `namespace_lifecycle`, `native_normal`, `native_timeout_kill`, `strict_modes`, `assessment_policy` and `independent_audit`; its exact `checks` set is defined by `REQUIRED_CHECKS` in that module. These booleans are a root-reviewed attestation only after raw receipt and lifecycle audit; they are not themselves evidence. Envelope must bind all separate case and policy receipt hashes to this exact source and spec, with explicit passed/failed scope, rather than inferring one check from another. Retain failures and partial source/receipts. Release unused reservations only after root audit.
