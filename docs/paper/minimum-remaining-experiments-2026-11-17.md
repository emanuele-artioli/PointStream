optimization, a large preference study and the 192-frame egocentric reference
backlog are optional extensions. Equipment harms, temporal defects and background
costs remain visible under the narrower player-body/clothing-plus-ball claim.

does not negate generated-player evidence.

the stock facing failure under its paid body-only conditioning, without claiming
