# Author-review manuscript length audit

## Scope and decision

The preserved `690cad0` author-review package reports 23 pages of body and
references plus five appendix pages. The later contribution-ladder V3 and V4
packages report 18 plus five. These are builds of different manuscripts, not a
controlled estimate of how many words five pages can hold. V4 changes the human
reference pilot status from V3 without new codec results; the primary tennis
table and image remain unchanged. This audit reads the records and packages;
it does not modify or build the manuscript.

The reduction reflects a substantive change in narrative: the paper now leads
with the prospectively fixed ball-priority confirmation, competing controls,
foreground negatives and qualification limits. It is not solely the removal of
operational detail. Some scientific results and explanations were condensed or
moved outside the paper. A longer version could usefully develop the evidence
and its limits, but should earn its space rather than fill a nominal allowance.

## What moved and what remains visible

The V4 identifiable source ZIP contains
`supporting-material/v3-research-restructure/section-block-mapping.json` and
`evaluation-v2-verbatim.tex`. The mapping preserves nine entire V2 evaluation
blocks externally, including claims, figures and limitations, while recording
that selected scientific data were retained or reordered in the new main text:

1. Evidence selection and research questions.
2. Foreground-first tennis evaluation.
3. Ball visibility and paid reconstruction.
4. Saved native rate–quality curves.
5. Initialization and recurring background costs.
6. Appearance and correction costs.
7. Conditioning and measurement effects.
8. Tennis perception and egocentric reconstruction.
9. Computation and operational claims.

These names describe the archived blocks, not nine sections wholly absent from
V4. The V4 evaluation retains the three-source ball result, development-window
ROI and same-position controls, paid foreground and shape negatives, a
qualification table, and historical task rescue, harm and absence counts. It
also omits or condenses detailed historical analyses, figures and caveats from
the old blocks. The archived copy includes both scientific material and
operational history; it should not be described as a cut of administrative
content alone. The V3 restructure also moved related-work artifact-intake text
outside the build.

**External preservation is not reader visibility.** The verbatim files and
unresolved limitation dispositions are outside the paper build, not the five
pages of built appendix. They preserve evidence and provenance; they do not
by themselves communicate an explanation or limitation to a reviewer, nor do
they resolve it. Review any proposed restoration against current evidence and
the claim it supports rather than copying the old chronology wholesale.

## Priorities for a useful expansion

Subject to the actual required template and page budget, a provisional five-page
allocation would be:

- Around 1–1.5 pages for the frozen confirmation design: source selection and
  exposure, sparse visibility labels, candidate and control construction,
  rate accounting, metrics, reference envelope and sensitivity interpretation.
- Around one page for what the ROI and same-position pixel/streak controls can
  and cannot explain, alongside the full-frame quality costs.
- Around 1–1.5 pages for the most informative paid-foreground, shape,
  background or appearance/correction negatives, with a compact comparison of
  assumptions, outcomes and scope. Preserve the narrow positive beside its
  failure where supported; do not turn historical screens into confirmation.
- Around half to one page for historical task rescues, harms, absence errors,
  and source/judge exposure, clearly distinguished from the new perceptual
  endpoint.
- Around half a page for related work and the precise novelty boundary,
  especially regional allocation and competing explanations.

These are editorial priorities, not promises that a build fits or that each
historical result belongs in the final paper. Put operational job chronology
and exhaustive provenance in the artifact unless needed to interpret a claim.
Recheck all reintroduced text against its evidence and visible limitations.

## Page-budget qualification

The [format-access record](submission-format-access-2026-11-01.md) says the
legacy TOMM guide specified 23 body/reference pages plus up to five online
appendix pages in the small ACM format. It also records that current venue
requirements were not independently verified. The 690cad0 and V3/V4 counts
are author-review builds; they do not prove that five additional pages in that
layout are available under the current venue rules. Confirm the current review
template, page limit and appendix rules before setting a target, then rebuild
and inspect the actual final layout.
