# Research documentation navigation audit

This is a read-only audit of research navigation and source authority, not a
review of every pull request or a new scientific result. The current continuation
is [submission priorities](../submission-next-actions-2026-11-15.md); the
underlying experiment records, frozen contracts and accepted analyses remain
the authority for individual results. A later continuation can supersede
ordering without rewriting those records.

## Finding

The earlier roadmap and closed-stage language can hide a real, conditional
positive for generative players. It is now explicit at the top of PLAN and in
the current continuation and readiness amendments, but a reader following the
README directly to the older ladder or reading a historical closure in
isolation could miss it. Closure of a budget or fixed experiment is not a
finding that every generative approach failed.

The [Chapter 14 audit](../experiments/submission-ladder-chapter14-demo-audit-20261115.md)
joins the public manifest to the W7 player experiment. For al028 at W9's
corrected modeled two-hour price of 19.2398 kbps, player LPIPS/DISTS are
0.304/0.242 versus 0.384/0.303 for the best bracketed interpolated DCVC anchor;
full-frame LPIPS/DISTS are 0.285/0.175 versus 0.146/0.121. Lower is better.
That is a one-window, modeled-rate comparison, not a physically measured
exact-rate endpoint, BD-rate, human preference, all-frame temporal result or
complete codec win. The website's 18.46 kbps label used an underbilled reference
bank; corrected quality values are unchanged. The public manifest joins the
metadata but does not independently authenticate every served video frame.

The [repaired six-window W7 analysis](../experiments/w7-anchor-rate-repair.md)
reports conditional positive player-region gaps for familiar-match windows and
negative whole-frame gaps, with missing med000 DCVC and an inconclusive Medvedev
subgroup. Its window intervals are not independent-match population inference;
unseen-match Tier 2 remains inconclusive. Bank reuse, adaptation, receiver state,
framing and temporal qualification remain open. These limits and the positive
must travel together.

The fixed added-rectangle screen stopped with a scoped negative. The ball study
separately passes its registered narrow three-source perceptual criterion, with
full-frame costs, sparse visible labels and encoder exposure. Neither establishes
a combined winning player+racket+ball codec. Racket inclusion is a research
objective and dataset/prompt requirement; the reviewed current records do not
establish a separately qualified racket coding gain. Do not silently infer one
from player or ball evidence.

## Navigation and ambiguity

- README introduces the domains and links the ladder and readiness, but should
  link the current continuation first and name player, racket and ball as the
  tennis objective. Point to a concise scientific evidence map as the front
  door, not a historical job ledger.
- PLAN's current opening supersedes its older order, but its long historical
  body includes unqualified-looking statements such as the earlier bounded
  programme's “no further GPU experiment” and older closure directions. Label
  the historical section prominently with date/scope and link current authority;
  do not silently overwrite its evidence or prior decisions.
- [Components](../components.md) describes an intended architecture and
  [resources](../resources.md) still describes egocentric first, racket second.
  Mark these as architecture/resource background with their historical ordering,
  rather than current research priority. Keep dataset limitations intact.
- [Experiments](../experiments.md) provides protocol and historical decisions;
  link current prospective protocol and continuation. Its assertion that the
  agent context remains cheap for about an hour is not verified cache lifetime;
  avoid using it as an operational guarantee. The 45-minute recovery bound is
  separately specified and remains useful.
- Readiness now points to the player correction near the top, but its older
  recommendation that tracked pixels are the strongest tennis development
  candidate and that a generative route needs reopening can be read as the
  present decision. Qualify that paragraph as the pre-demo decision, superseded
  by the current saved-player audit and design review.
- A stopped fixed recipe, an unqualified receiver/cost question, and a positive
  result conditional on its observed scope are different statuses. Use those
  terms at each entry; never promote an unresolved gate to a failure or claim
  a stopped allocation is an endorsement of a different method.

## Minimal research front door

A short current science brief should present, in order:

1. The intended end-to-end tennis question: player, racket and ball together,
   native/neural and relevant simple controls, paid bytes and whole-frame costs.
2. A compact table of **qualified within scope / stopped fixed configuration /
   unresolved** for W7 player, paid ball, racket, fixed foreground and the
   complete system. Pair every benefit with its strongest cost or limitation.
3. The next decision: authenticate saved media and complete receiver/accounting
   details, then a source-spread, matched-rate or explicitly rate-labeled human
   pilot before choosing the next representation. Neither the visual pilot nor
   the existing positive authorizes a new compute run by itself.
4. Links to the current continuation and source analyses, followed by the dated
   historical ledger. Distinguish evidence, plan, permission and artifact
   status so an old stop cannot override a later research question.

Preserve dated experiment records, receipts, prior negative results and source
identities. If PLAN is condensed, make a lossless archival extract with stable
links and verify its contents before removing duplicate historical prose; do not
rewrite or discard the append-only scientific record. This audit did not move
history or verify all PRs. Review current portal rules and venue fit separately.
