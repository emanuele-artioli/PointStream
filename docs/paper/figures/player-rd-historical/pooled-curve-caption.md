Seven source-aligned tennis windows from three matches, averaged within each match and then equally across matches. NAD001 is excluded from all three methods because its neural low-rate point failed the exact-decoder gate. Each curve appears only where all seven windows have measured support for that method. Scores are interpolated linearly in log rate within each measured codec/scale family; the best supported ordinary AV1 family is used at each rate, with no extrapolation. The grey region is the sampled common support of all three curves; dots are interpolated at 33 kbps. W7 scores the original source-player-matte and pose-drawn union on every third frame. PointStream uses a modeled two-hour reuse charge for its appearance references and same-match adapters; this is not a verified complete broadcast receiver. Alcaraz–Hurkacz and Medvedev–Struff are seen matches with held-out clips; the Nadal–Kyrgios base had not trained on that match, while its adapter and reference bank used separate earlier match footage, and its court marks informed registration design. The three matches and their correlated frames are a descriptive sample, not an independent-frame confidence interval. A full eight-window comparison including DCVC-UF remains pending.
