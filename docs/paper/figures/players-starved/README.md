On the eight paired windows, the mean improvement in DISTS is 0.07344, 0.06652 and 0.06668 for the three matches, respectively. Equal-weighting those three match means gives 0.06888. This is a descriptive equal-match average across different modeled rates, not a win at one common bitrate or a population confidence interval.

## Reproduction

Run `MPLBACKEND=Agg python docs/paper/figures/players-starved/plot.py` with Matplotlib. It validates input hash, count, identities, support and rate brackets before generating the figure and result receipt. It uses no source images or GPU.

**How much of the historical win depends on duration?** In all eight paired windows the generated region had lower DISTS than even the highest-quality scored DCVC encode. For each window, price the original adapter, guider and modeled 12-crop bank over enough active seconds to reach the highest *measured* DCVC bitrate in that window. This takes 26.2–95.2 minutes under the original cost model. Al028 takes 26.3 minutes to fit its 28.8 kbps comparison; reaching 19.24 kbps still assumes two hours. These are sufficient modeled durations relative to the observed encodes. More expensive neural encodes could close the quality gap, and actual cache validity over those durations is unmeasured. The later 26.7-second calculation prices a different, pretrained two-image receiver at a higher 125 kbps target.
