## Proposed results text

We evaluated the foreground-first trade-off on previously unused 240-frame broadcast windows, generating players on every third frame. At each hybrid's total rate, we compared player-region LPIPS and DISTS with the best available SVT-AV1 and DCVC-UF anchor, defining the gap as anchor minus hybrid; positive values favor the hybrid. In six windows from two matches whose other footage trained the tennis base, the pooled player gaps were +0.103 (95% interval [0.041, 0.165]) for LPIPS and +0.085 ([0.057, 0.112]) for DISTS. The Alcaraz windows operated near 18 kbps at 25 fps and the Medvedev windows near 41 kbps at 50 fps. These intervals treat windows as independent despite their grouping within matches. The Medvedev subgroup alone (three windows) did not meet the decision rule: LPIPS +0.121 [−0.098, 0.340], DISTS +0.091 [−0.004, 0.186]. One Medvedev window lacked an accepted DCVC comparison and was compared with SVT only; omitting it leaves five windows with gaps +0.080 [0.066, 0.093] and +0.074 [0.062, 0.087], respectively. Two Medvedev windows also lacked a bracketing encode for one SVT ROI policy.

On three second-half windows from Nadal–Kyrgios, a match absent from the base's training, the corresponding gaps were +0.072 [0.005, 0.140] and +0.070 [0.048, 0.091], at approximately 43–50 kbps and 50 fps. A match adapter was fitted on three disjoint first-half clips; court marks from this match had previously informed registration design, but the generator did not receive the evaluation frames. The LPIPS interval clears zero by only 0.005, and three windows from one match provide limited evidence of generalization.

These are foreground quality gains accompanied by a whole-frame cost: the best available anchor had better whole-frame LPIPS and DISTS in every window. DCVC-UF was the strongest player anchor wherever available; the exception is the SVT-only Medvedev window. The comparisons bill each match adapter as if its bytes were amortized across a two-hour match, an assumed duration rather than a measured break-even point. They therefore do not establish the rate trade-off at shorter durations. Separately, the full-frame-rate W6 test found greater temporal flicker in the generated players than in the SVT anchor; the W7 player scores do not establish temporal stability.
