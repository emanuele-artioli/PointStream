## Current format and author inputs

The accessible [legacy TOMM guide](https://tomccap.hosting.acm.org/guide.html) says 23 pages for text/figures/references in a small ACM submission format plus five online-appendix pages in the same PDF. ACM's [LaTeX best-practices document](https://www.acm.org/binaries/content/assets/publications/taps/latex-best_practices-06-may-2020.pdf) instead explicitly instructs `manuscript` for review and describes journal output formats for production. These are primary guidance documents but not proof of the live TOMM-specific submission system. Attempts on 2026-11-01 to open [current TOMM authors](https://tomm.acm.org/authors.cfm), [ACM submissions](https://www.acm.org/publications/authors/submissions) and the Digital Library journal guidance returned inaccessible/403. Retain the current review class, which agrees with generic ACM review guidance; verify the live journal's review format, anonymity and system requirements before filing. Do not substitute fabricated publication metadata from the small-format probe.
