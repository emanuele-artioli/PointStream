**Why first:** the existing task result is more directly connected to a paper-visible object than another attempt at the stopped W8 receiver. 2. **Independent ground truth and held-out split.** Before scoring, identify *labeled* frames and source clips that were not used in the development selection or in training the judging model. Evaluate actual decoded images with an independently qualified output judge against frozen ground-truth annotations, with registered frame IDs, a prespecified spatial tolerance, visibility, all-frame denominator, misses, and false detections on ball-absent frames. Returning the known transmitted/rendered coordinates is not an image-based task evaluation. WASB is a candidate only on footage excluded from its training, such as its documented TrackNet games 8–10 split, after source-image qualification; the encoder-side BallTrack cannot certify its own drawn marks. The prior comparison of label, BallTrack and WASB **position sources** does not make BallTrack an independent judge, and WASB training overlap must remain disclosed. If no suitable independent labels/judge or untouched clips can be established, stop at that finding rather than claim confirmation.
3. **Pilot before scale.** Freeze a content-blind two-window pilot and a four-window untouched confirmation set as separate clips from the eligible pool, if enough distinct non-overlapping footage exists; do not force the counts by reusing a development or training clip. The pilot can establish validity and per-stage cost, not a confirmation win. Use native saved or newly generated source-matched SVT-AV1 and DCVC-UF rate points with common resolution/color, full frame coverage, complete actual byte counts and the same per-frame scoring masks/labels for both arms. Compare original codec and codec + streak (and the label-position oracle only as an explicitly privileged ceiling). Measure detection recall, false positives and missing outputs, ball-region quality, and whole-frame LPIPS, DISTS and PSNR at matched rate/overlap; report per-window results and no extrapolation. Any fitted reference, side information, correction and model adaptation must be included or distinctly amortized; cold and conditional warm costs must be separate.
4. **Budget and decision.** Before running a pilot, write a card specifying the exact eligible clips, independence checks, frozen payload, anchors, target metric, quality guard, hypothesis, competing bright-blob/judge-leakage explanation and finite smoke budget.
