## Decision and human-review rules

The [prospective protocol v2](experiments/submission-ladder-protocol-20261115-v2.json)
removes the magnitude floor for future scientific conclusions. Historical
10% development rules and their original outcomes remain visible. Reconsidering
a smaller observed gain requires replication and a new declared study; it does
not turn an old development observation into confirmation.

Before closing or promoting a direction on perceptual quality, prepare a
representative visual pilot for the user where feasible. A failed serialization,
cost, source-identity or other correctness gate remains a failure regardless of
visual preference. Record metric disagreement as a research question; keep
fidelity, naturalness, temporal coherence, identity and task accuracy separate.
Do not choose a favourable metric after viewing holdout results. Source-only
reference annotation and codec-output preference review are different tasks;
codec outputs must remain hidden during reference correction.

Perceptual quality can disagree with reconstruction distortion; that possibility
does not establish disagreement for PointStream. See [rate–distortion–perception
analysis](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/blau19a.html) and the [ITU-T P.910
subjective assessment guidance](https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-P.910/en). A one-person
pilot is a design diagnostic, not a validated multi-observer preference result.
