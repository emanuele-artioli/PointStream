# Candidate paper structure and reusable text

This is an integration proposal, not an edited manuscript. The [manuscript audit](domains/manuscript.md) identifies exact copy/commit changes; the [experiment audit](domains/experiments.md) determines which historical observations can enter a final table. The original two-window measured-tennis report was recovered during this pass; its rate comparison remains explicitly withheld. Unqualified historical values can appear in a clearly labelled development/limitations appendix, but cannot silently become accepted benchmark results.

## Two evidence-dependent directions

**Focused contribution.** A qualified candidate must demonstrate a favorable same-source operating point or overlapping curve under a declared quality/access contract, complete physical accounting and fresh-client reconstruction. If recovered or later established, organize the paper around the mechanism producing that gain, with this dossier supplying ablations, controls and limitations. There is currently no recovered confirmed broad PointStream win against AV1/VVC or the selected generative competitors. A task-specific result would require a task-specific title, metric, domain and claim.

**Broad empirical study.** Organize the existing work around research questions: available foreground headroom, cost of independently reconstructing foreground, background initialization versus recurring correction, sensitivity to conditioning and measurement contracts, and domain-specific perception/task requirements. Credible negative results and invalidation mechanisms are distinct outcomes. Connect literature methods to implemented adaptations, qualified probes and intake-only work; do not imply a benchmark replication from wrappers or paper summaries. This direction can preserve substantial work without claiming that every failed implementation demonstrates an algorithmic limitation.

## Draft paragraphs for adaptation

**Motivation.** “We investigate object-centric video coding by separating scene background from tracked foreground and corrective information. Conventional coding of foreground-removed tennis targets indicates an opportunity: the stored eight-scene diagnostic reports mean reductions of approximately 14.2–18.3% across four codecs. This changes the target and omits the cost of reconstructing removed objects. We therefore use it to motivate a budget question, rather than as a semantic-codec improvement.” [Source and arithmetic](records/coordinator-arithmetic.json).

**Rate boundary.** “A semantic prediction is useful for coding only when the receiver can reconstruct it from transmitted information. We account separately for background, appearance, motion and shape, correction, envelope and any adaptation/deployment policy. Rate–quality pairs use delivered decoder pixels from that same stream. A source-derived plate, source track or uncharged model may support an oracle diagnostic but cannot establish independent coded reconstruction.” [Implementation boundaries](records/code/non-pr-scientific.md), [rejected assembly claim](records/code/recovered-only.md).

**Historical curve result.** “Within the saved native Gate A observations, the candidate requires approximately 10.3% more rate than VVC over the common VMAF interval under the historical cubic integration. AV1's saved quality support is disjoint from the candidate, so we report no BD-rate for that comparison. A separate interpolation sensitivity retains the negative sign. These calculations reproduce stored curve observations; final benchmark promotion additionally requires certification of source, decoder and measurement provenance.” [Curve and overlap record](records/gate-a-derived.json), [sensitivity](records/gate-a-pchip-sensitivity.json).

**Negative-result interpretation.** “The tested modular routes often exchange improved regional reconstruction for appearance, alpha and residual cost that exceeds the reference budget. These outcomes bound particular windows, representations and checkpoints. They do not establish that generative reconstruction or neural coding is universally ineffective. Conversely, an invalid metric, train/inference mismatch or failed checkpoint integration cannot be counted as a negative algorithmic result.” [Campaign](domains/experiments.md), [conditioning/metric repairs](records/code/non-pr-scientific.md).

**Perception and domain scope.** “Tennis and egocentric video share useful observation contracts and coordinate-transform requirements, but differ in foreground structure, camera motion, guidance and downstream tasks. We retain those differences in data identity, mask evaluation and rate accounting. Detection/track coverage alone does not measure segmentation accuracy, and a development pilot does not establish a held-out codec result.” [SAM comparison](SAM31_RECONCILIATION.md), [demo](domains/demo.md).

**Research direction.** “The remaining opportunity is conditional: foreground recovery must fit the demonstrated budget, and background persistence must save more than recurring refresh and correction cost. A future favorable result should identify that mechanism through matched ablations and a fresh receiver, including the conditions under which it fails. Until then, the recovered study documents tested boundaries and provides explicit missing comparisons.” [Falsifiable gaps](GAPS.md).

## Replication labels to carry into the paper

Conventional native encodes with saved artifacts are **historical measurements**, with qualification status attached. Custom HNeRV-style, SPADE/Pix2Pix and representation changes are **adaptations/probes** unless a faithful replication packet exists. Pretrained foreground screens are **checkpoint-specific probes**. SAM 3.1 pilots are **perception/implementation studies**. GVC-RT, GLC-video and MTTF intake records remain **literature/source inspection** until checkpoint, execution, stream and receiver gates are passed. Failed imports or unavailable checkpoints are **integration/access outcomes**, not losses on a quality curve.
