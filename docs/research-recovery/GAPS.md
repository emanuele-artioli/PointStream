## What is boundedly closed, and what is not

- **Closed as evidence:** constant-table/grey-strip ladder victories, source-derived client reconstructions presented as independent decode, JE11 presented as measured consecutive-point persistence, invalid early LPIPS/VMAF/model-path rankings, and success inferred from accepted but inactive ROI controls. Their old interpretations must not return. Their implementation lessons remain useful.
- **Negative under tested conditions:** the saved native Gate A/VVC comparison, the specific measured capped modular points, and the reported checkpoint/window screens described in the experiment records. This supports a credible empirical account of tested limitations.
- **Still open:** different qualified checkpoints, truly held-out matches, task-specific metrics, complete fresh-client candidates, longer real sequences, and improvements that alter the identified cost mechanisms. Calling these open does not predict a win.

A promising direction needs both a mechanism and a missing comparison. Foreground headroom suggests that appearance/motion/residual cost is the bottleneck to test; repeated initialization suggests a duration hypothesis only if recurring cost stays below the anchor. Egocentric task preservation suggests a separate quality target only if clip identity, judge independence and all-frame failures are controlled. These are falsifiable proposals, not paper conclusions.
