## Separate historical and derived analysis

[The curve figure](figures/gate-a-stored-curves.png) draws saved observations. The historical cubic log-rate integration reproduces about **+10.30%** versus continuous VVC and **+10.32%** versus segmented VVC over their overlapping VMAF ranges. The separately labelled [PCHIP sensitivity](records/gate-a-pchip-sensitivity.json) gives about **+14.09%** and **+13.88%**, respectively. Both signs are negative for the candidate. This sensitivity is a new CPU analysis of old scores; it neither replaces the historical verdict nor independently certifies source frames or the metric.

No AV1 BD-rate is reported because quality support does not overlap. A lower sampled bitrate endpoint cannot establish a codec-wide bitrate floor. An absent overlap is an unsupported comparison, not a candidate win or an algorithmic negative.

## Admission checklist for a future pooled table

Before putting two points on one figure, verify their source hashes and window/split, target and information access, actual delivered decoder pixels, checkpoint/adaptation policy, complete rate boundary, metric regions/version and operating conditions. Missing values remain missing. Native failures, mismatched inputs or invalid instruments move a point to the methodological record rather than a performance ranking. Timing requires uncontaminated comparable hardware and processing scope; historical timings in different environments are descriptive only.
