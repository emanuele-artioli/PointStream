# What the recovered research body can support

PointStream has enough recovered work for a substantial empirical study of object-centric semantic video coding. It does not yet have a confirmed broad codec win against AV1/VVC or the selected generative competitors. The strongest paper would explain where apparent foreground headroom is consumed by independently reconstructing appearance, motion, background and correction, while separating valid negative measurements from failed instruments and unsupported historical claims. A focused favorable contribution remains conditional on a qualified matched result. [Paper proposal](PAPER_MAP.md), [code correction chain](domains/code.md), [experiment assessment](domains/experiments.md).

The recovery brings together manuscript history, active and archived demo work, full code/PR lineage, shared and host-local server artifacts, saved campaigns and prior research chats. It preserves positive diagnostics, bounded negatives, corrections and missing evidence. The code union contains 1,165 commits and all 149 PR heads; the paper union contains 63 commits. Those totals describe recovery, not 1,228 independently certified scientific changes. Net PR behavior and development stages were reviewed in groups; individually unreviewed intermediate patches stay visible. [Code inventory and review scope](domains/code.md), [manuscript copies](domains/manuscript.md), [coverage](COVERAGE.md).

The clearest motivating evidence is the conventional foreground-removal diagnostic. Its eight 4K, 48-frame scenes from six matches report mean coding reductions of approximately 14.2–18.3% across VVC, AV1, HEVC and AVC. Independent CPU arithmetic reproduces the stored means and scene standard errors. This changes the target and does not pay for recovering removed players; it supports an opportunity question, not a PointStream gain or a causal allocation of “player bits.” That distinction gives the paper a credible starting point and a concrete budget to test. [Experiment report](domains/experiments.md), [source and arithmetic](records/coordinator-arithmetic.json).

The saved native Gate A curves supply a bounded negative comparison. Historical cubic integration gives approximately +10.30% rate against continuous VVC and +10.32% against segmented VVC over their shared VMAF support. A separately labelled PCHIP sensitivity gives about +14.09% and +13.88%, retaining the negative sign. Saved AV1 support is disjoint from the candidate, so no AV1 BD-rate is defensible. These are checks of stored scores, not new frame rescoring or native recertification. The original comparison and the new sensitivity remain separate. [Curve assessment](COMPARABILITY.md), [figure](figures/gate-a-stored-curves.png), [derived record](records/gate-a-derived.json), [sensitivity](records/gate-a-pchip-sensitivity.json).

The modular campaigns are valuable because they expose costs rather than merely announce losses. Coded crops, color, alpha, masks, pose, plates and residuals form a substantial set of controls. Some points improve a historical regional-quality aggregate while greatly exceeding the anchor's cost; tested capped points fail to satisfy both axes. A ghost-control probe also shows small measured improvements while missing its prescribed target; preserve that partial positive separately from invalid boundary fields. Model screens are narrower: an AnimateAnyone checkpoint failure or exact-video Pix2Pix capacity probe does not close its model family, and per-clip learned weights belong in the rate/access policy. Preserve original metrics, source windows and checkpoint conditions instead of pooling all rows into one leaderboard. [Campaign records](domains/experiments.md), [July adaptation and correction cards](records/code/midyear-direct.md), [comparison groups](COMPARABILITY.md).

Several apparent victories are useful methodological findings. JE10's component arithmetic sums to 64,840 bytes against a 65,149-byte anchor—a nominal 309-byte margin—but the retained runner reconstructs with cached background and source tracks rather than proving a persisted fresh-client stream. JE11 multiplies repeated costs rather than measuring distinct consecutive source intervals. Earlier table-derived ladder victories used supplied scores and grey strips. These claims cannot enter a benchmark as wins, but their component work and the precise reasons for withdrawal belong in the development record. An implementation or instrument failure must not become an algorithmic negative. [Code evidence](records/code/recovered-only.md), [independent arithmetic](records/coordinator-arithmetic.json), [manuscript claim reconciliation](domains/manuscript.md).

The manuscript copies contain complementary material and contradictory claims. The Mac ends in August, Overleaf contains the September ladder promotion, and GPU3 adds another commit plus dirty corrections. The audit reads and preserves all three without synchronization. Visible “outcome-safe” routing from smaller payload alone, generator-ranking prose inconsistent with its own tables, and unmeasured multi-match/second-domain claims require withdrawal or reconciliation. The reusable material is the scene factorization, receiver/accounting contract, qualified controls, motivating diagnostic and explicit limitation markers. The two-window replacement report has now been recovered and its arithmetic checked; it expressly withholds a rate comparison, and full-envelope/fresh-client qualification remains missing. [Measured-row audit](domains/experiments.md). [Copy and commit audit](domains/manuscript.md), [proposed integration](PAPER_MAP.md).

The egocentric demo supplies a distinct task and representation domain. Its moving camera, hands, object manipulation, neural background and task judges require their own input, exposure and accounting contracts. The added tennis SAM 3.1 chat and core-idea chat belong in this audit: they can share observation adapters, coordinate transforms, transport-parity lessons and quarantine policy with the egocentric work. They cannot certify each other's masks or quantitative performance. Tennis track coverage is not segmentation accuracy; an inactive development manifest is not a qualified training split. The demo audit finds incompatible retained factory clip names, unlike hand-instance/frame denominators, raw-pose reconstruction despite counted quantized packets, and foreground pixels retained in the background. These findings qualify its apparent gains. [Detailed source paths](records/demo/live.md), [history and clip alarm](records/demo/history.md). [Demo report](domains/demo.md), [SAM reconciliation](SAM31_RECONCILIATION.md), [prior-session recovery](domains/sessions.md).

A broad paper can be organized around five research questions: available conventional opportunity; foreground reconstruction cost; background persistence and correction; sensitivity to conditioning and instruments; and domain-specific perception/task quality. Its related-work table should say which systems were actually replicated, adapted, probed or only inspected. Existing GVC-RT, GLC-video and MTTF intake is useful preparation, not executed comparison evidence. The paper can state that optimization remains open when it identifies a falsifiable cost mechanism and the comparison still missing. It should not call an entire family exhausted or promise that more optimization will necessarily win. [Section and draft-text map](PAPER_MAP.md), [sessions and survey verification](domains/sessions.md).

The highest-value next step is a complete fresh-client test with matched source, metric and charged stream. Then test foreground recovery under the demonstrated budget and background persistence on actual consecutive intervals. An independent all-frame egocentric task comparison is a separate route. Training, long-sequence evaluation and a new broad survey remain outside this recovery. Prioritized hypotheses include their prerequisites, tested coverage and estimated effort. [Ranked gaps](GAPS.md).

Recovered histories and selected raw artifacts are retained externally, while lightweight records and analysis belong on the scoped branch.
