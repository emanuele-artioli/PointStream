## Remaining scientific holes, in priority order

1. Persist complete packages and prove a fresh receiver consumes only them; exact registered frame hashes and complete physical-wire/adapted-weight costs are the highest barrier. JE10/source-assisted demo cannot bypass this barrier.
2. Freeze common source windows/splits, region masks and metric implementations. Keep source RGB/Y quality, regional weighted dB, generated-reference MAE, perception agreement and task scores separate; qualify independent truth/missing detections.
3. Matched actual codec ladders and suitable overlapping support. Conventional anchors must share input/access/latency contract; component savings, cheap endpoints and higher-quality expensive points cannot replace them.
4. Repair active generation conditioning and qualify checkpoint/training exposure, pose/racket wires and masks before generation-on interpretation. An implementation failure is not an algorithm-family negative.
5. Close targeted orphan/deeper payload/native decode provenance gaps. The July 11 orphan, unreviewed family contents, masked-target/alignment manifests and absent grid report remain transparent searches, not invented outcomes. Historical timing across hardware/cache states is descriptive.

Bounded closed routes: retire constant-table victories, Gate B pass, JE persisted/session headline, the 94% claim with unserialized geometry saving, unlike-denominator oracle percentage, unmeasured pose OKS/real-time prose, and broad HNeRV/AA rankings. Preserve actual observations and components; no route closes the general object-centric hypothesis.
