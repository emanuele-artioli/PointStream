The main reusable idea is a measurable decomposition: background representation, object appearance/motion/shape, and optional corrective residual, with conventional coding and simple coded/warped foreground controls. Changing a component changes prediction and transmitted cost together. The all-disabled raw passthrough control is not an encoded anchor. Delivered-client pixels must be paired with actual complete bytes. Architecture tests and causal packet coding are narrower than a causal live end-to-end system.

The correction chain is scientifically useful. Foreground-removal/background-only BP21 measurements motivate an opportunity on changed targets. Their eight-scene mean foreground-removal reductions (14.2–18.3% across codecs) do not measure recovered-player cost or whole-scene quality. Later negative paired/whole-codec rows show that more bytes can buy more quality without a rate advantage. Direct manuscript inspection also finds contradictions independently of the code audit. At the Mac revision, the claim that static copy beats all generators on object PSNR accompanies 11.82 dB for static versus 12.03–12.21 dB for generators; a same-offset LPIPS row gives AnimateAnyone .570 versus static .582 while saying it does not clear the floor. At Overleaf's September revision, C1's 33.0 weighted dB versus VVC's 35.2 weighted dB is called a 65.4% victory from the byte ratio alone; even accepted literally, that row does not establish dominance or BD-rate. The source-code audit additionally invalidates its constant-table measurements and grey visual strips. The later dirty patch itself withdraws the constant-table claims, unmeasured Pose OKS and unencoded second domain, replacing them with reported two-window WebP-still rows: C1 is 528,958 B versus VVC 112,295 B at 48 frames (35.6 dB foreground but 20.5 dB overall versus anchor 31.3 dB), and 2,883,316 B versus 258,024 B at 192 frames. These are uncommitted source reports, not independently certified replacement results. They also differ from other campaign weighted-quality rows; retain both identities rather than merging their metrics.

The newest system design still visibly calls smaller-payload routing “outcome-safe,” while its HOLE says never-worse BD-rate is unproved and its historical G1 chose fallback on every observed chunk. Smaller bytes are not a quality guarantee. Keep each historical assertion, contradictory row and later correction in the detailed cards.
