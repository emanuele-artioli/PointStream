# Modern modular, journal and perception experiments

Audit date: 2026-09-30. This is a read-only recovery of the September E04/E05/E06 campaigns, the JE05/07/10/11/12/16 modular journal line, and the Sep 25–27 mask/SAM pilots. No experiment was run, no original source or output was modified, and no new result is claimed. Evidence is joined from selected files under workers/server/outputs, source/code.git history, and the preserved C01/R02 dirty-source snapshots. The scratch capture is rawworkers/experiments/modern; its inventory records source paths and SHA-256 for copied files and git-show extracts.

## Finding

These sources preserve useful bounded component diagnostics and transport work, but do not support a modern PointStream rate–quality victory. Keep the reported observations with their exact window, target, metric and receiver assumptions. The strongest positive-looking headline, JE10, has reproducible nominal arithmetic but no self-contained receiver package. E04A is a one-scene background-only comparison; E04B directly measures only 2.5–4.9% ghost-MAD reduction and is rejected by the later policy audit for the standard pipeline, while the residual error’s physical cause remains unresolved. E05 rejects one trained Pix2Pix configuration; a later completion JSON asserts that blank conditioning was tested, but the preserved parser audit and lack of a distinct control corner do not support that assertion. E06 stopped its tested full-codec configuration; its lossless mask-RLE floor arm is smaller than VVC at much lower inherited quality (20.68 vs 24.6 dB). SAM player masks and YOLO mask-screen results can be compared against a shared imperfect cached mask on one identical 16-frame window, but neither cache agreement nor a model-only mask smoke is ground truth or codec evidence.

### E04B same-camera paired removal

The pre-registered probe pairs the saved removal-off panorama at QP 47 and 32 with two removal-on candidates, using mask exclusion plus nearest-finite/Telea fill. Its criteria require at least 50% ghost-MAD reduction, at most 0.5 dB visible PSNR loss and at most 15% total-package byte inflation. Saved pre-measurement bounds are copied at workers/server/outputs/selected_reports/evaluation-20260914/e04b/run-20260916-paired-removal-r2/bounds.json and workers/server/outputs/selected_reports/evaluation-20260914/e04b/run-20260916-paired-removal/bounds.json.

I retrieved the primary r2 report, derived audit and retry/provenance ledger read-only into rawworkers/experiments/modern/retrieved/e04b/. At QP 47, removal-on is 4,519 B, 21.467 dB visible PSNR-Y and ghost MAD 8.715 versus off 4,554 B, 21.161 dB and 9.165 MAD: only 4.91% less MAD. At QP 32, on is 16,469 B, 22.826 dB and 6.538 MAD versus off 17,411 B, 22.798 dB and 6.704 MAD: 2.48% less MAD. Bytes fall 0.77% and 5.41%, PSNR changes +0.306/+0.028 dB; sender time rises from 1.879/1.967 s to 4.207/5.349 s, while client time rises about 0.031 s. These do not meet the registered 50% effect threshold.

The direct paired report records both ghost-MAD bounds as alarms and an `INCONCLUSIVE_EXPLORATORY` verdict because the registered policy did not define a hard stop for 0–20% reduction. The later derived audit reproduces the canonical OFF metrics exactly and computes boundary MAD: OFF 12.694/9.968, ON 11.763/9.988 at QP 47/32. Thus the paired report’s boundary fields of 0.0 are defaults, not measurements; the later audit supplies measured values. Its error decomposition finds removal changes the lossless plate ghost-MAD only 5.208→5.135 (1.4%); coded VVC adds 3.580/1.403 MAD to ON for QP47/32. The roughly 5.14 uncompressed residual is an observed error of this single-homography pipeline, not a proven geometric limit; parallax, sensor noise, lighting, registration and interpolation remain unseparated. Zero inpaint holes means Telea was not exercised.

The retry ledger resolves the apparent two-directory ambiguity: attempt 1 encoded both ON arms, then failed report serialization; attempt 2 reran both, completing in 32.56 s. All four QP bitstreams/side-data files match between attempts, so there are four encode invocations but two unique encoded arms. The cumulative 60.56 CPU seconds includes an approximately 28 s estimate for the failed attempt; the r2 run measured 32.56 s. Run code was base aefdeb2, dirty=true, diff SHA-256 60bb3a…befb7a4e; a later committed script is bitstream-equivalent, but the runtime diff digest itself cannot be reconstructed from Git. The later policy audit classifies removal as **not promoted/rejected for the standard pipeline**: 2.5–4.9% reduction is below its clarified 20% meaningful-effect threshold and does not justify 2.2–2.7× sender time. This rejects the tested policy, not the cause of residual plate error.

**Status:** validated negative for the tested standard-pipeline change after the later policy clarification; causal attribution of the remaining error is unresolved. No second-camera test or new candidate encode is part of this evidence.
