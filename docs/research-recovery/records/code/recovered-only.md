### JE10: genuine executable work with an unsupported full-receiver headline

`1a9a90f8339fdd7965c0b2b89a0245823d18ae4c`, despite its subject “assemble ... winning full codec,” changes only `docs/areas/evaluation.md` and `docs/strategy/journal-experiment-plan.md` (29 added/14 removed lines). The executable runner arrives in the next commit, `a4f603729e7ab7ac78800ed1142b49dfd5cbb153`: 289 lines in `experiments/modular/je10_full_codec.py`, plus 34 test lines. This ordering is an explicit negative result from source inspection: the subject alone would falsely identify the implementation revision.

The JE10 runner really encodes per-object color videos through native AV1 and decodes those files through the codec wrapper. It also constructs and counts motion bytes and lossy silhouette bytes. Its nominal arithmetic is therefore a meaningful candidate-accounting observation, not proof that no encoder work occurred. However, the claimed self-contained receiver is contradicted by the processing path. Background is loaded directly from `np.load(bg_path)` while its charge is the constant 24,648 B, split into constants 22,906 and 1,742. Motion tuples retain source-derived boxes/presence; the compositor recomputes `_boxes(track)` from the source mask tracks rather than decoding stored motion wires. Silhouette arrays are decoded immediately in memory; their payloads accumulate in local lists without being written as a complete client package. Only AV1 color files are persisted in `wires/`. The final reconstruction starts from the cached decoded background. Its “background_sha256” hashes that cache, not a transported background stream.

The VVC cap (65,149 B), scores (25.3664 weighted dB), encode/decode times and background timing contributions are constants; foreground timings are measured during the call. Thus the reported comparison mixes contemporary candidate operations with historical anchor/cache constants. Unit tests of silhouette behavior cannot establish background transport, fresh-process reconstruction, full-envelope bytes or timing parity. Status: **implementation present; nominal candidate arithmetic observed; full-codec victory invalidated/retracted; prospective codec outcome unresolved**, not an algorithmic negative result. Root independently reproduced 64,840 B total, 309 B slack and the historical weighted arithmetic in `../coordinator-arithmetic.json`.

`efc7b15017fac8bbbed342a64117b4a59ebf629c` adds 17 paper-area documentation lines promoting this candidate to a winning headline and causal ablations. `3725388ebadb14f09c046657082d187b7735786d` adds further evaluation/journal claims for JE11, JE12 and JE16. JE12's contrasts may motivate ablations, but the doc additions do not themselves establish controlled executions. JE16 reports 66.09 seconds of canvas rendering and 69.75 seconds total client work alongside a 29.58-second VVC decode: these numbers also cannot support a speed win, even before timing provenance is certified.

The correction is preserved in the prior research ledger pinned at `b427edff5146df0b14060c05681e7bcb7e1237fb`, `docs/research/08-evidence-ledger.md`: JE10 fresh client incomplete, JE11 extrapolation, JE05 noncausal pixel diagnosis and JE07 conflicting summaries. Its authority is a later source audit, not a new experiment. The original promoted claims remain recoverable here so that their retirement is traceable rather than silently erased.

## Other recovered work and exclusions

`4e9bcba16a28c79e6c7e17e4c571827263f206ee` adds `src/components/metrics/track_geometry.py` (205 lines) with 179 test lines. It scores already-associated `(T,K,2)` landmark coordinates using strictly increasing presentation timestamps, pixel tolerance, visible-reference denominators, missing detections, precision/recall and adjacent-frame velocity error. It rejects partial NaNs, invalid visibility and timestamp sequences. Missing predictions cannot improve hit-rate denominators, and velocity is never bridged across missing frames. This repairs a *metric capability gap*: it does not detect/associate court lines or balls in RGB, establish calibration, or supply measured court/ball accuracy. Status implementation present, empirical result unresolved.
