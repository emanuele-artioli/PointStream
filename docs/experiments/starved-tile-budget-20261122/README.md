All 240-frame low-cost tile encodes and serialized sidecar round trips passed. The CRF36, 48 and 60 tile variants cost 63.503, 56.345 and 52.293 kbps with valid cached player references, or 467.365, 460.207 and 456.155 kbps cold, respectively. The budget-selected variant at 52.293 kbps attained player-mean DISTS 0.2593 and ball DISTS 0.2956. The 52.113 kbps 480 × 272 plain AV1 stream scored 0.3975 and 0.3431. Both 480 × 272 ROI neighbors at 51.840 and 53.002 kbps also scored worse on these objects. Whole-frame RGB PSNR was 23.602 versus 26.947 dB for plain AV1. These are 12 ticks from one opened rally, and a 0.35% running-rate match under ideal reference reuse. It is a regional win under that rate model.
