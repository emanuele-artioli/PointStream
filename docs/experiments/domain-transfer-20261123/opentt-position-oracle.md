## Measured result

| Delivery | Total bytes | Delivered kbps | Ball DISTS ↓ | Ball RGB PSNR ↑ | Central RGB PSNR ↑ | Whole RGB PSNR ↑ |

| Ordinary 320×180 AV1, CRF 60 | 15,819 | 63.276 | 0.26990 | 20.827 dB | 11.396 dB | 29.131 dB |
| Candidate base alone, CRF 60.25 | 15,520 | 62.080 | 0.26731 | 20.832 dB | 11.394 dB | 29.098 dB |
| Same base with the paid trajectory | 15,763 | 63.052 | 0.24754 | 20.956 dB | 11.808 dB | 29.099 dB |

PointStream used 0.354% fewer total bytes than ordinary AV1 and reduced mean ball-crop DISTS by 8.28%. It improved on ordinary AV1 at all 85 marked instants, and on its own underlying base at 82. The central PSNR gain of 0.412 dB is larger than the 0.129 dB gain over the full ball crop. Whole-frame PSNR decreased by 0.033 dB. The latest comparison isolates a transferable **representation** on one fixed camera and one interval with official positions. A receiver can parse those same positions directly. A domain-qualified sender and independent localization across intervals remain separate evidence.

The two complete physical replays on each coded stream gave the same YUV hash on all 240 pictures. PSBL consumed 152 bytes for the positions, presence and appearance and another 91 for its wrapper. Max coordinate quantization error was 0.472 pixels on the 1280×720 display. All 155 frames without a transmitted symbol were visually identical to the candidate base.
