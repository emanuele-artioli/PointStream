## Primary claim and racket extension

The new primary region comprises visible player body/clothing and visible
ball pixels; specify this before the next outcomes and use the same source-only
reference for all methods. Excluded racket pixels remain in full-frame scoring
and, under the70/30 objective, the complementary region. Do not call this an
all-object foreground win or imply that equipment fidelity is solved. Historical
W7 masks/means and ball crops remain separately named, unchanged observations.
Do not recompute a favorable historic headline under the new target after the fact.

A racket-inclusive secondary audit on a fixed source sample can reveal harms,
but no racket improvement is required for the player-and-ball claim. Native
fallback may preserve poor racket pixels without improving them; disclose that.
The fact that the baseline also struggles is a hypothesis to inspect, not proof
that deleting a racket or changing its geometry is harmless to viewers.

Existing RacketVision labels include five racket landmarks and a box. It is
straightforward to serialize coordinates, but coordinates alone do not specify
appearance, blur, occlusion or a reliable generator. Independent source/frame
alignment and labelled visibility still precede any equipment conclusion. First
test a paid small coded rectangle or a coded reference/planar-warp candidate,
with metadata, reference, refresh and residual costs. Use actual reconstructed
pixels and an explicit equipment endpoint. An ideal source-pixel paste is an
exploratory diagnostic, not a universal LPIPS/DISTS upper bound. Train a racket
model only if a bounded pilot shows a specific need and a feasible gain; no
new racket network belongs on the current critical path. An appendix can report
a small correctly scoped pilot without promising a complete equipment codec.

## Small contemporary requalification before choosing a model

1. Join saved source, preparation, generation, checkpoint/adapter/base and judge
   manifests. Check per-unit/profile/timestamp completeness, actual masks and
   each comparison's rate support. Label invalid comparisons unresolved and
   publish a new correction; never overwrite original scores or old verdicts.
2. Check crop/pose coordinate transforms, RGB/channel order, model-specific
   reference/pose preprocessing and train/inference tensors against the pinned
   upstream model path. Synthetic correctness tests cannot establish model
   quality, but catch a disadvantaged generic baseline before GPU comparison.
3. Freeze a representative exposed development subset, paid reference/mask and
   common native-cadence contract. Compare only three existing profiles:
   released generic weights, common tennis-only weights, and the existing
   match-adapted recipe. Authenticate each profile; match references, poses,
   seeds, temporal context and quality settings. No retraining is needed for
   this initial comparison. Do not use lower-quality sampling settings as
   evidence of the intended production generator's quality or cost.
4. Include strict coverage, actual decoded inputs, comparable native/ROI/neural
   controls, complete paid-state costs, temporal/object failures and a separate
   source-spread human pilot. A changed preprocessing recipe is a new candidate,
   not a rerun that silently replaces old evidence. Use paired seed controls;
   add seed replication if stochastic instability can change the decision.
5. Select the cheapest reliable profile supporting the stated objective; report
   effects and source-level uncertainty without a10% magnitude floor. Freeze
   the final candidate before independent-match confirmation. Do not tune on
   the already opened W7 or three-source ball confirmation cohorts.

## Extra sports: source screen now, at most one inference pilot later

The [primary-source domain screen](additional-sport-domain-screen-20261115.md)
compares skating, volleyball and soccer. A lower actor count makes skating a
promising cheap transfer test if usable prior GenStream RGB can be recovered.
Start with an actual SVT-AV1 base containing the background and charge all its
bytes. Do not assume the client has a rink/3D scene, omit scene provisioning,
or build a new3D reconstruction system for the first pilot. GenStream's old
scene-provisioned result cannot be copied as a PointStream codec gain.

SportsMOT provides a concrete volleyball/football alternative with declared
720p/25fps sequences and player boxes/IDs. Those are not actor masks or ball
labels. SoccerTrackv2 provides a further openly documented panoramic-video
candidate; many tiny people, camera projection and the ball remain harder than
the category names imply. More foreground area can increase pixel-codec
expense, while also increasing pose/identity/reference/occlusion costs and
receiver compute. Compare the total, not foreground area alone.

First qualify continuous RGB, source/cadence/rights, exact labels and actor count
using metadata and a source-only inspection. Choose **one** added sport only
if it reuses the qualified common pipeline and fits a separately costed small
inference stage; no new model training or bulk corpus download by default. Use
one mechanically selected short native-cadence development sequence and three
rate points, actual paid background/reference/metadata, relevant ROI/neural
anchors and the same declared foreground objective. Inspect temporal/occlusion
failures and human views; full-frame costs are reported, not an automatic win
requirement. One sequence is exploratory transfer evidence. Only a promising
case admits independent-source evaluation, never a broad cross-sport claim.

Stop the extension if source access, annotations or a representative complete
runtime cannot fit the remaining schedule. A domain screen can change which
single pilot has best value, but must not become a second open-ended project
before the tennis player-and-ball construction works.

## Paper decision

Keep a sports-led journal as the working structure. Egocentric results can
remain a bounded comparison of where tested representations failed or narrowly
helped, if they answer the same scientific question under clearly distinguished
protocols. The amount of past effort alone does not make a section or another
paper coherent. A separate egocentric paper is reasonable only if it has its
own defensible contribution, adequate evidence and author agreement. Do not
launch two publication projects now or treat a split as a substitute for evidence.
Decide after the requalified tennis pilot, before final manuscript scope freeze.
