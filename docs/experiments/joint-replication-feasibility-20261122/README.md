# Independent-match joint replication: feasibility audit

Decision: **do not start a GPU replication yet**. The bounded inventory did not find a different physical match with all of (i) all-native-tick outputs from the same common-tennis two-player receiver, (ii) authenticated cleaned background and exact paid conditioning, and (iii) independent, source-aligned player supports and ball points on common ticks. A result made from the remaining inputs would not reproduce the new Nadal–Kyrgios 21.545 kbps running-stream comparison. This is a missing-data/qualified-pipeline finding, not a negative experiment or evidence that no such data exists. No new GPU or codec run and no result-selected source or proxy figure was produced.

**Best existing lead: Alcaraz–Hurkacz 028.** W7's selection record identifies a different physical match, source SHA256 `c51dace59349914da401356984de4e83da658c878011db3f54ff233077152934`, 25 fps, 240 source frames and 9.6 seconds, with source masks and keypoints. Its `@120` prepared-window identifier is not permission to use a different frame offset; the archived selection says source clip start 0. The W7 generation archive `20261030T150651Z-a58b2ce2/full/published.tar` (SHA `d88f1c79cd78f292dc70105540cde4b659a4726138d34de02f343b4bb9edbd91`) contains two real player NPZs; the score archive `20261030T185245Z-e51a4dec` contains numerical scores. A read-only campus tar inventory confirms the two generation members and separate scores. Those outputs cover **80 every-third-frame ticks**, not all 240 native ticks. They use W7's Al-specific adapted checkpoint `ah-C`, not the new joint result's fixed common-tennis model `85e3c65c…` and source-denied all-frame replay. W7 player masks are source-matte/pose-derived fixed supports, not independent human-review body masks; its main S region is a union of source mattes and warp support, not separately reviewed silhouettes. No independently labeled ball coordinates aligned to this exact source and time were found in the bounded repo/manifest search. Same-match fit/conditioning overlap needs requalification. W7's corrected 19.2398 kbps is a modeled two-hour reference/adapter reuse result and omits some operational framing, not a paid 240-frame common-model joint stream. These constraints are documented in [W7](../w7-untouched.md), the [Al028 audit](../submission-ladder-chapter14-demo-audit-20261115.md), and the [existing proposed joint study](../starved-joint-20261122/README.md).

**Other physical matches and RacketVision.** W7 has 240-frame source windows on Medvedev–Struff and other Alcaraz–Hurkacz clips, but its nine historical comparison windows likewise use every-third-frame generation/scoring, matched or match-adapted conditioning, source-derived region supports, and no verified independent ball labels joined to the same source frames. The RacketVision release provides native-cadence clips, sparse official visible/not-visible ball labels and racket boxes/keypoints, but **no body masks** ([inventory](../../resources.md)). Its named candidates (including the separate Basel and Adelaide machine tests) have no authenticated two-player common-model carrier, donor-cleaned base, or reviewed body supports. The bounded source-only scoreboard audit does not establish an Al028 or Medvedev–Struff clip/frame correspondence, and different IDs or differing hashes do not rule out re-encoded overlap. Do not transfer ball labels across footage without a verified frame-exact join; do not call source-model masks human ground truth. The release metadata says MIT, but individual broadcast-footage rights for a publishable new figure remain unresolved.

**Why the 2 GPU-hour / 3 elapsed-hour threshold is not established.** The historical W7 stage estimate for one window is 4,654 GPU-seconds (about 4,284 on Ada), including generation, receiver masks and scores **for the every-third-frame path**, before new native-rate generalization, source-denied requalification, a new cleaned base, independent ball labeling and repairs. Naively scaling its 1,735-second generator and 820-second mask terms threefold already exceeds 7,200 GPU-seconds for those terms alone, but it is only a rough scaling estimate, not a valid wall-time forecast. The separate Nadal 240-frame receiver had different assets/hardware conditions and about 55 estimated worker minutes for generation/masks plus requalification work; it is useful context, not proof that a new Al or RacketVision pipeline meets this threshold. Queueing, annotation and fit isolation remain unmeasured. The condition for authorizing the full experiment is therefore not met.

**Smallest credible next unlock.** Pick one source *before seeing outcomes*, authenticate the original frame sequence and installed rights, independently mark both player silhouettes and ball centers (including explicit unobservable status) on the same fixed 12 source ticks, and retain unknown ticks as unknown. For Al028, reuse the actual source-derived W7 masks only as preliminary prompts and obtain independent review; independently align any ball annotation, rather than using the fitted sender as its own judge. Then a source-only, best-case clean-background/compositing pilot at the target byte budget can test alignment and show whether the proposed reconstruction is worth a full run; explicitly state its free/oracle assumptions and do not treat it as a mathematical bound on all perceptual metrics. Only after a 24-frame native-rate common-model generation/mask smoke, its saved checkpoints, per-stage GPU timings and a 1.5× full-run forecast fit both limits should the 240-frame study be released. Report exact cold initiation and already-paid-cache assumptions. Absent those prerequisites, keep the 21.545 kbps joint claim on its one development rally and the older W7 player-only evidence separate.
