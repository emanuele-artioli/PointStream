**Question and bound.** What mask rate can the existing H5p 4a composites afford if the exact same reduced union mask and every other bit and score are retained? Rebill the same frozen weighted-DISTS-selected rate points three ways: ideal zero-bit mask, ideal 2 kbps total carrier cost, and the lowest-byte exact-roundtrip shape stream actually measured for each item, including the replacement container tag/length. All candidate masks are required to recover the existing reduced mask exactly, so the zero-bit case dominates every nonnegative-cost carrier of that same mask on those fixed pixels and stream points. This does not bound a changed foreground/background stream, arbitrary segmentation error, learned mask, or a new rate/quality operating point. In particular, an inaccurate mask can improve a perceptual metric, so do not assert monotonicity across different composites.

**Rule.** Against the venue-adapted DCVC-UF anchor and separately the pooled achievable anchor envelope, compute weighted DISTS BD-rate with PCHIP over the common quality range. The paid exact-mask route warrants a new separately scoped carrier pilot only if all four items have comparable curves, mean BD-rate is at most −10%, and at least three items are below zero against both anchors. Otherwise stop this exact-pixel fixed-point route; report each item, missing overlap and the paid bit components. Also report weighted LPIPS as a separate limitation, not a second claim of success. Do not extrapolate from one small-foreground item to VISOR. Frozen scores and inherited F, generic/kitchen models and anchors remain as historically measured, including all exposure and omission limitations in H5p/H5q; this is not a new codec or reconstructed-frame result.

**Hypothesis:** the zero-bit ceiling can pass DISTS but real shape payload and 2 kbps are unlikely to retain the full pooled-envelope rule. **Competing explanation:** the venue and pooled hulls and an item-specific allowance leave enough margin that a ≤2 kbps exact-union carrier can retain the DISTS result. **Caps:** 0 GPU seconds; ≤120 one-core CPU seconds including archive identity and validation, plus a ≤30-second preflight. This analysis does not authorize a carrier, segmentation, decoding, scoring, or further GPU work.
