### Price of a fixed 33 kbps common comparison

The PointStream upper-rate windows that need new higher-base points are al034 (30.894 kbps), al028 (32.350) and al043 (32.7, exact value in the figure receipt); the other five paired windows already span 33. The separate DCVC limitation is larger: the strongest accepted Al DCVC points top at 28.829, 19.369 and 20.026 kbps respectively. Each Al window therefore needs at least one real, independently decoded higher-rate DCVC packet exceeding 33 kbps on all 240 source frames, scored on the original 80 ticks with original W7 region support. These new points must bracket 33 with an already measured lower point. Their actual bytes, not a short-frame prediction, decide whether they cover the target; a further QP may be necessary.

The closest relevant original W3/W7 al034 DCVC job `20261031T113203Z-cec062e0` used 92.8 GPU-seconds for smoke and 222.1 for two full points, on RTX 6000 Ada, including dual-process decode checks.
