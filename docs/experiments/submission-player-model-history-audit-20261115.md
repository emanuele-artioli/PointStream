# Experiments: player model history audit

### 2026-11-15 — Generator, reference, receiver and fidelity evidence

This is a read-only inventory of recorded studies, not new quality, training or codec evidence. It separates player appearance, temporal fidelity, racket fidelity and ball transport. The user's player/racket/ball objective needs each: a sharp generated person does not establish a correct racket or ball. The evidence below is scoped to the stated inputs and accounting; historical headline outcomes do not imply a complete deployed codec.

**Generator adaptation ladder.** [K6b steps 2–5](k6b-players.md) used Moore-AnimateAnyone and the same six development windows/four matches with a granted per-player reference crop and granted target-region matte. At the keypoint-rate pixel envelope, generic zero-shot `original` won 3/6 LPIPS and 3/6 DISTS (50%, failed ≥75%); three Djokovic windows won while both Alcaraz and Rublev windows lost. Judge jobs `20261022T182340Z-e773acb4` and `20261022T182347Z-c0aac9cc`, code `47db732`; six complete windows, two unjudged because they could not change the verdict. It did improve against a reference warp across these windows, but that is not an equal-rate complete-codec result.

A rank-64 per-window LoRA fit on the evaluated frames improved LPIPS on all six and won 4/6 LPIPS and 6/6 DISTS (83%, oracle pass): the target frames were training input, reference/matte were granted and roughly 49 MB fp16 LoRA was unbilled. It bounds the potential of this fit, not generalization. Rank-4 per-match fitting on other shots won 4/6 each unbilled and 3/6 LPIPS plus 4/6 DISTS billed over two hours (58%, failed), while improving most windows versus zero-shot. The cumulative step-5 C recipe adds more match data, a rank-64 sport base, a trained pose guider and 4-bit rank-4 match adapter: 4/6 wins on each metric (67%, failed), despite lower two-hour model charge. `alcaraz_hurkacz/045` and `rublev/052` still lost both metrics; the latter worsened and guidance versus fitting was not isolated.
