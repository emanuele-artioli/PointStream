# Same-job exclusive 16-frame conditional cost worksheet

This is a forecast, not a measured fit or authorization. The previous scenario used shared 16-frame numerical work 316.386 s multiplied by 1.5 for exclusive timing (474.579 s), 183.214 s staging, 30 s validator, 5 s no-op and 50 s source overhead: **742.793 s of the 800 s campaign**, leaving **57.207 s** for all additional same-job retention, hashing, publication and forecast error. The multiplier is an assumption, not a measured exclusive runtime. The 580 s namespace timeout and 600 s smoke limit separately constrain their respective scopes; the assumed numerical component alone leaves 105.421 s within 580 s, before any other work inside that namespace. These are necessary arithmetic bounds, not guarantees: new retention I/O, source copying, staging, publication, NFS contention, validator extraction, startup and cleanup may overlap or be charged in different scopes. Stop on timeout or cap; do not infer a larger budget.

At 16 frames, six retained receiver YUV/RGB pairs total 398,131,200 raw bytes. `retain` copies the actual receiver files into the prepared checkpoint and verifies hashes; original work copies remain through scratch. The real parity run moves both prepared and scored trees into `publish`; prepared contains one retained receiver set, while score assembly deliberately skips receiver-evidence and the marker. Normal fleet publication archives this `publish` tree and hashes the NFS archive; it does not nest the salvage-only checkpoint archive. Retention adds at least one local read plus write of 398 MB, verification/sealing rereads, and one publication read/write plus NFS archive hashing; exact pass counts, files, padding, additional artifacts and throughput are not measured. Validator extraction/verification may add reads. The 57.207 s is an aggregate maximum allowance for all new overhead and forecasting error, not an independently measured allowance for each pass.

The released prospective caps are 1,000 GPU seconds inclusive (800 campaign, 200 post-campaign), 1,200 CPU seconds inclusive (30 preparation already precharged, 1,170 future), including 370 external CPU seconds after preparation; cover failure, publication and audit inside those bounds. The existing 32 GiB local declaration is scoped only to this 16-frame test, with fleet's separate 50 GiB local free reserve. At 586 frames the known floors alone are 39 GiB prepare and 37 GiB score before extra files, caches and reserve; 32 GiB cannot fit either. No measured 16-frame fit proves 48, 292 or 586. Assess actual published archive membership, byte sizes, stage times and high-water before any scale decision; the older 3,350-second pair estimate does not include this retention path.
