# K5d full-30 analysis and reporting contract

This pre-result reporting plan makes the registered [K5d protocol](../k5d-ball-benchmark.md) easier to reproduce; it changes no scientific criterion, selects no result, and does not claim the benchmark has run. Apply it only to independently accepted, source/schema/output-joined full-clip results. The [selection](study-selection-20261104.json) and original protocol remain authoritative.

- **Population and units.** List every one of the 30 registered clips from games 8–10, including the three historical source-judge failures, with its frame count, source qualification and support status. The fixed population is 5,675 frames: 5,472 visible, 197 explicitly absent and six unknown/occluded. Unknowns are neither visible nor negative. All clips were used in tracker selection: describe the analysis as retrospective. The 16/48 prefixes and the 188-frame full Clip3 are nested observations, not extra clips; count an accepted full Clip3 only once. Show per-clip results and descriptive game summaries, identify the three games, and do not call the 30 clips independent matches or assert a precise population confidence interval from three games.
- **Task endpoint.** Report visible hits and visible denominator, explicit absent-frame false detections and absent denominator, source-qualification failures, unsupported settings, and coverage per clip and game. Keep all 30 in the endpoint denominator; a failed source or unsupported low point cannot pass. For each clip show the two fixed low-rate candidate settings, native-envelope support and paired recall differences. Preserve the registered requirement of at least 75% of all 30 clips qualified with supported mean recall gain at least 0.10 across those two settings, alongside the separate pooled excess-false-detection criterion of at most 0.05 per explicit absent frame. The latter uses all 197 negatives and requires the registered native-rate support; report missing support rather than inventing a value. The historical source false detections **10/197** exceed 0.05 and must appear beside positive task findings; they block a strong absence/realism claim. Distinguish source qualification from the independent fidelity route.
- **Curves and rates.** Retain per-clip paired operating-point tables and curves, with descriptive views grouped by game; show actual available support and missing clips. Use the prespecified achievable native envelope and the original PCHIP overlap rules: minimum shared quality span 0.005 for LPIPS/DISTS and 0.1 dB for PSNR, at least two distinct valid points, and no extrapolation. When overlap is insufficient, report the supported ordering or unresolved range rather than a BD-rate. Report whole-frame metrics and the original DCVC PSNR BD guard separately from pointwise nondecrease. The primary comparison uses direct native IVF/PSDC bytes for the anchor and complete PSBL+BALL wire bytes for the candidate; show both native and wire accounting. Zero-BALL PSBL is only a labeled sensitivity, not a replacement anchor.
- **Controls and interpretation.** Keep the original source-versus-decode PSNR guard and calibration-only wrong-position/brightness stress controls with their diagnostic status and bytes visible; source-pixel/label-placement oracles are not deployable codec results. Distinguish independent WASB task evaluation from BallTrack encoder-model consistency and report source-pixel-assisted sender fitting. PSDC carries its native stream metadata, but decoding also depends on fixed shared conventions and the identified decoder/model context; do not imply a self-contained, universally portable decoder. See the [sender and transport audit](sender-native-transport-audit-20261109.md).

The saved [analysis helpers](../../../experiments/tennis/k5d_benchmark_analysis.py) check exact 30-clip membership and fixed label totals, preserve source-failed and unsupported statuses, and calculate the two-setting excess only where native support exists. Their output and any descriptive aggregation still require the registered artifact, byte, source and provenance audit; a helper pass alone does not establish a valid result.
