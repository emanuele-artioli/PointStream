### 2026-10-24 — Oracle design, before GPU execution

- The CPU audit exposed a card assumption that is false: K6's feature
  segmenter requests only `person`, `player`, `racket`
  (`k4b_features.PROMPTS`). K6 judges players and touching rackets, not the
  ball. All eight windows' ball plane is therefore empty because no ball
  masks exist in that input, **not because the ball is absent from the video**.
  K4's older ball masks were transient (not saved in its published outputs).
  The CPU run below is valid player/racket accounting, not joint-F accounting.
- User scope choice requested in the item chat: regenerate ball masks through
  the existing SAM path inside the same 4 GPU-hour budget (recommended), or
  explicitly scope the oracle to players/rackets. Until resolved, no joint
  mask rates or joint oracle verdict can be reported. No GPU has been allocated.
- Proposed fixed oracle: **rate relaxation**, not an assumption that replacing
  foreground pixels with source pixels must improve LPIPS or DISTS. For the
  finite pipeline below, encode/decode the actual foreground and backgrounds,
  form the actual composites, and keep their measured quality. Set only the
  foreground stream payload's price to zero. Retain all mask bytes, background
  bytes, and container/header bytes. The oracle dominates exactly those
  configurations: their reconstructions are identical and their rates cannot
  be lower with a nonnegative foreground payload. The grant is explicitly not
  deployable. A failure closes only this coder/grid, not every possible split
  or a key carrier whose shape cost is not the explicit MASK payload.
- Pipeline to bound: H5's mask decoder at s=8, source extraction from the
  decoded mask, push-pull foreground padding and H5's alpha ring, identity
  enhancer slots; off-the-shelf DCVC-UF HT-L foreground at three settings
  (QP 0 at quarter size, QP 21 at half size, QP 42 at full size).
  Background: Telea-filled foreground as the fixed removal method, separately
  coded with off-the-shelf SVT-AV1 and DCVC-UF. No venue fitting. Report s=16
  mask-price sensitivity only as such until its changed reconstruction is run.
- Initial background/anchor grids (same grid for both, full transmitted
  duration): SVT-AV1 preset 1 at CRF 62/full, 62/half, 55/quarter;
  DCVC-UF HT-L QP 0/quarter, 21/half, 42/full. Three coarse settings per
  coder, no six-setting sweep. The achievable pooled upper concave hull is
  the primary anchor; per-codec hulls are also reported. If this sparse grid
  cannot bracket shared quality ranges, the verdict is unclear, not a fail;
  add points only inside remaining budget. This is preset 1, not K6's preset 4.
- Quality: weighted LPIPS and DISTS (0.7 foreground, 0.3 background), frame
  LPIPS/DISTS and weighted PSNR, identical score frames for all arms:
  K6-registered frames with foreground, every third frame as in K6 for
  perceptual metrics. Both masks' provenance and the ball-data scope must
  be recorded. Rates cover all 240 transmitted frames, never just registered
  seconds. No BallTrack detection is a native ground-truth ball label.
- Rule fixed here before any GPU run: a metric passes if mean per-window
  BD-rate is at most −10% and at least 6 of 8 windows are below zero against
  the pooled envelope. Primary metric: weighted DISTS; weighted LPIPS must
  be reported separately and must not inherit its verdict. BD-rate uses
  PCHIP of log rate over the overlapping quality interval. Non-overlap is
  reported qualitatively as better/worse at every rate, not a fabricated
  percentage. If a mean cannot be computed because of non-overlap, do not
  turn qualitative wins into zeroes: report unclear and inspect coverage.
  A pass permits a proposal for billing the real foreground stream; stop for
  the user as the card requires. A fail stops this scoped grid; report
  background, mask, and foreground rate components and what it does not bound.
- Hypothesis: the explicit mask price (despite F around 1%) may consume
  the margin at the low-rate points. The source of the possible gain is
  separate rate allocation around the players, not foreground area alone.
  Competing explanation: the filled background still spends nearly the same
  bits as the whole frame, and padding/ring artifacts remove the quality gain
  even when the foreground payload is free.
- Budget partition: at most 0.5 GPU-hours for missing ball references if
  approved, 0.25 for correctness smoke and one-window cost pilot, 2.75 for
  the remaining oracle windows, 0.5 held for retries/extra curve points.
  Total never exceeds 4 GPU-hours. Each stage at most 45 min and each
  finished window is saved. No scale promotion before a measured pilot
  projects the remainder inside the remaining budget; revise scope or stop
  if it cannot fit. Native paths/versions, all selected input and patch
  checksums, model–GPU compatibility, deterministic DCVC decode and source
  identity checks are required in every run. This design has not been run.
