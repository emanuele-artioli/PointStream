# Historical quality reuse with delivered MKV rates

Root accepts the original 53 quality arrays as retained historical measurements associated with delivered MKVs whose decoded native YUV is proven equal to the historical decoded native YUV. This approval covers those 53 rows only. The [row-level review receipt](historical-53-reuse-review-20261105.json) records each score, source, mask, model, native-YUV, MKV, proof and independent-validator identity, billed bytes and GPU class. It also binds the nine historical ready, specification, dispatch, scorer and runtime records. The original historical join, raw receipts and failed or unlaunched gates remain unchanged.

The independent review rehashed the published score archives and all 53 finite 4×80 arrays, matched the 53 unique row identities to their historical stream and native-YUV hashes, and rehashed each delivered MKV and its two proof records in the actual 14f7 publication. Every independent validation reports ordered-payload, timestamp, re-extraction and native-YUV checks with the pinned DAV1D decoder. Historical source and mask inputs, LPIPS and DISTS weights, source-YUV expectations, ticks, code compatibility bridge and packed runtime are retained in the evidence chain. Each container's rate uses its full MKV bytes and its declared cadence.

The 48 original W1 rows ran on A6000; the five W9 repair rows ran on Ada. Nine paired controls spanning three windows and three scales used identical historical native-YUV hashes; their A6000 and Ada 320-value arrays differ by at most 0.000118. The med000 shared-scoring pilot reproduced its A6000 control's 320 historical values exactly. These observations do not bound every row. Final conclusions require explicit sensitivity to ±0.001 in the quality metrics, without treating that value as a statistical guarantee or changing existing quality gates.

Historical RGB hashes were not archived. Equality of RGB produced by applying the historical conversion to identical YUV under its original processing environment is an inference, not an independently measured new-MKV RGB comparison. These retained numbers are not fresh neural scores for the delivered containers. Cross-class effects and the original score provenance must remain visible in results.

The proposed narrower cohort retains the four originally specified new points, counting the already measured med000 CRF70 point once, and proposes two med000/med019 scale-2 CRF63 controls for the remaining work. This is a prospective reduction of redundant controls, not a change to the original objectives, denominator, or point set. The former 22-score forecast failed and remains unlaunched. This receipt approves no further scoring, GPU work or budget; any five-point work needs its own complete cost, identity and admission review before launch.
