# W7 shared-model scoring pilot candidate

Purpose: measure a complete two-point W1 scoring path and test historical control parity before considering the 22-score run. The fixed med000 points are the original scale-8 CRF63 control and the new scale-2 offset-minus-20 CRF70. The control exercises the original numerical guard; CRF70 was chosen before looking at new quality results because its delivered stream and native pixels were independently established by the transport oracle. Both use 80 fixed ticks and one LPIPS and DISTS load in a single process. Every completed control must pass the existing W1 control tolerance.

Input proof is the SHA-identified 14f7a33e publication `55739efe3cbf6937b6251e26c2ed1322663f8d1d2d1ee5f36bfb1ace593cdb59`, with its independently audited 75-role map `fd53ad6f70b589e1849cd94ede731c1259867b48d0e2b80cf64fcd52dbc5377e`. The pilot re-extracts and hashes actual proof, independent-validation receipt and billed MKV from the publication. It binds the historical native-YUV digest, exact source/mask/hybrid archive hashes, source conversion digest, ticks, pinned models, and reviewed W1 scorer bridge. The output retains whole MKV bytes, fps and actual rate. Prior failed gates remain failed.

Proposed maximum is 350 GPU seconds and 450 CPU seconds inclusive: campaign up to 300 seconds, plus at least 50 GPU and 100 CPU seconds for external staging/audit; smoke at most 240 seconds and the validator and no-op full stage each 20 seconds. This is a ceiling, not a prediction, and reserves no compute. A complete 22-point forecast must include the pilot already spent, six independently supported source-context bounds, one full-study model setup, 22 times the maximum measured per-point cost, 1.5 margin, staging and validation/audit reserves, and fit the remaining 1800 GPU / 1670 CPU seconds. A complete pilot alone does not admit the 22-point run.

The 53 historical scores are not reused by this candidate. Their separate admission requires SHA-bound evidence for each old scoring record, source RGB conversion, ROI masks and all 80 ticks, LPIPS/DISTS and scorer/helper identity, in addition to the delivered native-YUV proof; a native match alone is insufficient. No source-specific cost receipts or full-score approval are assumed. A usable pilot spec still needs independently SHA-verified local and staged copies of the historical med000 source, mask, hybrid, windows/state and metric weights. No GPU job is authorized by this card.
