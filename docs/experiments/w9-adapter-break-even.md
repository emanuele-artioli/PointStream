## What is and is not paid

The historical PLAN calculation holds the reference-bank charge fixed at its 7,200-second value while varying adapter-plus-guider bytes over L. In W7, `w7_rates.py` charges 12 reference crops estimated by scaling the window's two PNG crop bytes, while `k6b_real_base_judge.py` also charges base IVF, keypoint packet and JSON bootstrap over the 240-frame duration. The modeled rate is therefore `R_7200 - 8*A/(1000*7200) + 8*A/(1000*L)` for adapter A bytes. An all-assets sensitivity instead uses `R_7200 - 8*(A+B)/(1000*7200) + 8*(A+B)/(1000*L)` for estimated bank B bytes. This is still an accounting sensitivity: reference reuse and its 12-crop scaling are assumptions, and adapter, model and stream framing must be measured before claiming a complete operational bitstream. W5 specifically calls its historical rate an optimistic floor because tensor bootstrap and framing were unmeasured; its curves must not be relabeled fully billed. The bank bytes and complete W7 result archives are not in this checkout, so a numerical all-assets result and W7 tier-level L* remain uncomputed; do not substitute the historical values.

## Critical correction to W7 reference-bank billing (2026-10-31)

The transport archive lists **two physical reference crops per window**, each with four alternative encodings (PNG and JPEG 70/85/95): eight `reference_files` entries. The frozen `w7_rates.rates` divided the total PNG bytes by eight, treating codec variants as crops, despite the intended 12-crop-bank model. It billed 1.5 times the two-crop PNG sum rather than six times: **the modeled bank was underbilled fourfold**. Source identities and all original W7 evidence remain unchanged. The corrected `w7_rates.py` counts PNG entries and validates their summed bytes; mixed-variant regression verifies the intended count. A two-hour corrected rate adds `4.5 * two_crop_png_bytes * 8 / (7200 * 1000)` kbps. This corrects the model, not the missing operational framing or unverified assumption that twelve crops with these sizes maintain quality.

The three original transport archives were independently SHA256 verified: jobs `20261030T150852Z-d4e7cd41`, `20261031T091524Z-7d2f62ea`, `20261031T171359Z-aba3845f`; exact archive hashes are in `w9-inputs/w7-archive-hashes.json`.
