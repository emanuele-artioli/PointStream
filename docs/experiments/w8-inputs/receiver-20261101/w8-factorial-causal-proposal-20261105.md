# Prospective W8 CUDA isolation-factor diagnostic

Status: design only; no runnable factorial worker or launch authorization has been produced. The portable W8 paired diagnostic and the GVC control are retained as observations; neither identifies the cause of a historical model failure.

Question: on one exclusive Ada GPU using the authenticated JCV runtime, do user/network namespaces, the exact portable W8 Landlock read policy, or their interaction correspond to CUDA initialization failure? Pre-register four fresh processes in fixed order: `(namespace=no, Landlock=no)`, `(yes,no)`, `(no,yes)`, `(yes,yes)`. Each performs the same CPU-only torch bootstrap, affinity, thread, file-descriptor and CUDA-not-initialized checks, followed by the same synchronized 1024-float `2*x+1` kernel and finite sum 1048576. The no-Landlock cells may open and close the five protected files and aliases for identity/access probes but may never read their bodies. The two Landlock cells use the exact portable normalized historical allowlist and must demonstrate denial of each of those paths and aliases. The supervisor alone authenticates the five original protected files and records an inode/sha256 receipt. Never load weights, models or inputs in any arm.

Hypothesis: the four-cell pattern distinguishes a namespace-dependent, policy-dependent, or interaction-dependent failure under these experimental conditions. Competing explanations include runtime, driver, ordering and other changes; a result does not establish a past cause or qualify generation. Failure or CUDA error 2 is a measurement when all four independently recorded cells and controls complete, not proof of physical OOM. Incomplete or misconfigured cells are inconclusive. Record actual namespace identities relative to parent, loopback condition when applicable, Landlock ABI and handled mask, same physical device UUID, torch/driver/device information, each argv, stdout/stderr hash, exit status and timing. Compare memory fields using their documented semantics and tolerance rather than assuming driver-reported total equals CUDA-usable memory.

Prospective limit: one exclusive Ada on gpu5 or gpu6; one CPU; four 45-second hard-limit arms; at most 260 seconds in stage, 300 seconds total fleet campaign, and inclusive 400 GPU-seconds/600 CPU-seconds including external work and release. Every cell needs enough time and campaign reserve before starting. Cost exposure, protected-data provenance, dispatch runtime identity, all outcomes, incomplete measurements and cleanup must be audited. There is no model, decoder, encoder or scientific quality claim.

A runnable harness is not yet qualified. The historical bootstrap always demands changed user and network namespaces and loopback-only networking, so calling it directly for the other three cells either fails or changes more than the intended factor. A separate harness must reproduce its CPU warmup, environment, FD/thread checks and actual archived `_restrict` operation exactly, then substantively validate all four receipts; its supervisor must handle hard timeouts without escaping fleet teardown and preserve incomplete receipts. Do not submit a four-cell specification until those requirements, current protected-identity and staged-environment fixtures, and measured staging/receipt costs have been reviewed. The four-cell 260-second stage forecast remains unverified.

Implementation candidate for independent review: `experiments/tennis/w8_cuda_factorial.py` and focused CPU tests in `tests/tennis/test_w8_cuda_factorial.py`. The spec is generated from the previously admitted portable spec with `python -m experiments.tennis.w8_cuda_factorial make-spec --deadline <UTC> --output <new-path>`; the generated spec must be reviewed and frozen with a real deadline. The previous paired measurement's 4.254/3.990-second arms suggest a prospective estimate of 4 × 1.5 × 4.254 + 100 seconds staging/protected hashing + 30 seconds validation/release = 155.524 seconds, subject to fresh cache and contention. These historical numbers are a forecast, not qualification of the new bootstrap. Four 45-second arm limits, staging and reserve are hard-stop controls; skipped, failed-setup or timed-out cells make the factorial measurement incomplete. CPU mock tests do not establish namespace/Landlock behavior or CUDA operation on the fleet; independent review and admission remain necessary.
