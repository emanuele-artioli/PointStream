### 2026-10-31 — W11: independent source-racket oracle feasibility (prospective; no experiment run)

- **Question and target.** At W1's paid hybrid operating point (22.737962 kbps on Al `alcaraz_hurkacz/031@129`), could a racket path materially improve the player and whole-frame result? First identify an independently defined, pixel-accurate visible-racket mask on the actual W6 source frames, including racket pixels detached from the player mask. Paste the aligned visible source pixels at zero added rate into the saved hybrid, with correct visible occlusion; score player S and whole-frame LPIPS/DISTS, plus the existing W6 temporal/experience measures. Report an independent racket task judge separately if one is available; building that judge is not a prerequisite to measuring this perceptual diagnostic. Record labelled player-frame coverage and exclusions and compare the same frames against the unchanged hybrid. Zero-rate source pixels are an optimistic oracle, not a deployable rate point; because perceptual metrics are not monotonic in pixel accuracy, it is not a mathematical upper bound on every possible renderer.
- **Prospective exploratory decision rule, requiring a valid annotation source and an assigned experiment budget.** On the paired, labelled W1 ticks, call the path worth further study if source-pasting reduces mean player LPIPS or DISTS by at least 0.001 and a 95% paired moving-block bootstrap interval for that reduction clears zero; report both player metrics, both whole-frame metrics and the available temporal/task judges alongside it. This threshold does not establish a paper claim. A null can gate a component only after showing that the oracle dominates that specific component, covers its visible pixels, occlusion and target operating point, and rules out the threshold. Otherwise it remains a diagnostic. Report the labelled subset beside full-window coverage; sparse exclusions limit the claim. The label source is unresolved, and this inspection budget does not cover a new experiment.
- **Hypothesis.** A precisely recovered visible racket yields a measurable player-region or task gain at W1's operating point. **Competing explanation.** Its pixel footprint is too small for the chosen perceptual metrics to move, even if equipment visibility improves; uncertainty, sparse labels, and occlusion may also prevent a valid full-window conclusion.
- **Inspection budget.** Zero GPU; at most 120 workload CPU-seconds for read-only inspection, authorized separately by the parent. No label creation, tracker, rendering, paste, experiment job or new GPU allocation is authorized by this entry.
