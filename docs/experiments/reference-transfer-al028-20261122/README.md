## The prespecified AVIF Q90 transfer

Both native jobs completed with original CUDA kernels, identical GPU UUID, model, weights, per-track seeds, pose rasters, boxes and tick sequences. Q90 used 54,461 image bytes versus 95,298 for pixel-exact lossless WebP, saving 42.9%. Original lossless PNG cost 157,438 bytes. The actual measured envelopes were respectively 57,017, 97,850 and 157,517 bytes. Across 48 source-model body regions the Q90 and lossless DISTS values were 0.27002 and 0.26918. The 0.00084 increase lies within the registered 0.01 margin. The two bodies at the available human-accepted tick scored 0.27479 and 0.27211. Small differences went both ways over time, with lower DISTS for Q90 on 23 of the 48 track-ticks. Body RGB PSNR was 14.114 versus 14.149 dB and source-flow luma error was 27.668 versus 27.696. Across arms the scored body coverage was at least 96.90% of each source proposal, with both crops restricted to their delivered boxes.
