# Hypothesis-driven experiments

Use the [evaluation contract](../research/06-evaluation.md) and [ordered experiment cards](../research/07-experiment-plan.md). This page preserves reusable operating principles, not dated campaign authorization.

Before running, write the decision, hypothesis and competing explanation; the source/split and immutable evidence reused; paired controls and changed axis; exact code/model/tool identities and command; complete byte and metric policies; client/time/memory limits; maximum configurations and elapsed budget; output location; and promotion, stop and inconclusive rules.

Default first diagnostic: ten minutes of evidence/instrument triage, then at most six new configurations and thirty minutes including scoring. Estimate from an existing comparable measurement. Reserve at least one third for controls and diagnosis. If the question cannot fit, write a costed smaller or larger card before proceeding. Installation and implementation get explicit separate budgets. A historical job budget is not standing authorization for repeated runs.

Reuse compatible saved decodes before encoding again. Check frame identities, transforms, component settings, code/tool versions, metric path and wire accounting. A new code fix does not validate an old artifact. Do not turn exposed development sources into confirmation data.

Count disjoint physical bytes for background, foreground, motion/masks, correction, and remaining envelope. Score the independently decoded delivered stream. Diagnose which component consumes the measured rate/quality margin before proposing another architecture. Background-only and source-mask oracles are diagnostic controls, not whole-codec results.

Compare simple paste, temporal coded crops, ready small models and ready generative models under declared rate and client budgets. Do not infer speed or fidelity from model family or parameter count. Separate plausible appearance from source/action fidelity. Qualify model-specific inputs and training before ranking models; unavailable or broken baselines are untested, not inferior.

Stop on wrong input, hidden source dependence, uncharged data, empty native output, ineffective options, failed metric controls or contaminated timing. Preserve evidence and classify each card as supported, contradicted, inconclusive, invalid or deferred. No quality overlap means no BD-rate comparison; do not extrapolate or switch metrics after seeing the results.

Every multi-hour preprocessing, training or evaluation run needs a bounded representative smoke through the same entry point. Inspect and dispatch from the Mac using [setup](../setup.md) and [long jobs](long-jobs.md). Record actual costs and the next decision. Publish negative results with the same scope and provenance as positive ones.
