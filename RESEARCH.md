# Research brief

PointStream asks when sending the motion of an object helps more than spending the same bits on its image. Its receiver can use one delivery to reconstruct the scene for a viewer and expose object data to an application. Keep the architecture general. Use tennis to establish where each representation earns its bandwidth. [Paper figure plan](PLAN.md)

Seven windows from three matches support all three curves. Two matches informed training, and the third used adaptation on separate earlier footage. Each method uses the same 80 sampled ticks per window. Rates interpolate within measured support and charge the historical appearance bank and same-match adaptation over two hours. PointStream and ordinary AV1 also cover an eighth window, averaging 0.249 and 0.401. ## Submission decision

Rewrite around scene-level choice, foreground fidelity and direct access to motion. Ball-only reconstruction fit within the tested clip’s playback duration on a laptop. Player generation took about 16 minutes per person for 4.8 seconds of video on a campus GPU. Describe that mode as offline. Alcaraz–Hurkacz gives a second physical match for the joint receiver. Its footage already informed development. Report this replication on its own terms and freeze the method before testing a fresh match for a general foreground claim. Racket grip remains visibly unreliable. A source-triggered racket crop recovered three of six labeled rackets at IoU 0.5 on one compressed clip, below the four needed to justify encoding those crops.
